Feel Good Friday: Find your curl.

One place you're forcing. One honest question. One thing to do less of this weekend.

That's the whole practice.

You don't need to add anything to your life right now. You need to stop flailing at one thing.

The champion surfer doesn't muscle the wave into submission. He finds the angle where the wave does the work, and he rides it. That's Wu Wei. Not passivity. Not giving up. Alignment.

This weekend: pick the one thing. Ask whether you're with the current or against it. Then, just for two days, try doing less with it instead of more.

Notice what opens up.
