## FAQ Schema
**Q: When is the Sheep year in Chinese astrology?**
A: The Year of the Sheep (also called Goat) follows the Horse year. In the current cycle, the Fire Horse year is 2026. The Sheep year begins in February 2027.

**Q: How does the Fire Horse year prepare you for the Sheep year?**
A: The Fire Horse year's volatility and intensity surface what is true in relationships and decisions. The clarity gained through difficult conversations, honest self-examination, and patterns named becomes the foundation for the quieter, more growth-oriented Sheep year that follows.

**Q: What should I focus on in love during the Fire Horse year?**
A: Use the Fire Horse year's clarity to say difficult truths, name real patterns, and either deepen what's worth keeping or clear what isn't. The Sheep year rewards what was built deliberately, not what was simply endured.

**Q: Is the Sheep year good for love?**
A: The Sheep year generally favors depth, partnership, and emotional connection more than the Fire Horse year does. For relationships that have been honestly worked on during the Fire Horse year, the Sheep year is a time of growth and consolidation.

**Q: What is the difference between the Fire Horse and Sheep years in love?**
A: The Fire Horse year (2026) is about revelation, speed, and volatility. It exposes what's real. The Sheep year (2027) is about nurturing, depth, and building partnerships. What you clarify in the Horse year grows in the Sheep year.
