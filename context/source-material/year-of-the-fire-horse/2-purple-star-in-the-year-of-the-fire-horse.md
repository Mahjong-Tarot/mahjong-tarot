# Purple Star in the Year of the Fire Horse

The fire horse year moves fast and amplifies everything. There will be high highs and low lows, some fantastic achievements and some total chaos. The positive side is real and great things can be accomplished, but this is not a year to take chances or get swept up in all the action. Preparedness is everything. Purple Star helps you decide where to put your attention and how careful to be.

## Know Which Palace Is the Focus of the Year

The most important question is which area of your life matters most this year: your job, your marriage, your children, or something else. Because the year goes by quickly, you need to know where to direct your attention first and make sure you are fully ready for whatever happens there.

It is just as important to know whether that palace is auspicious or inauspicious for you, because the fire horse magnifies everything. If the palace is auspicious, you can do great things. If it is inauspicious, you have to be even more careful than usual.

## Know Your Weak Palaces

Identify your weak palaces and treat them with extra care this year. Weakness is not always obvious. A card reading can tell you what is going on for the year, but knowing how strong or weak each area of your life is adds a layer the cards alone do not give you.

## Watch the Health Palace in Particular

It is easy to focus so hard on one challenging area, such as work, that you forget the others, and health is a common blind spot. The fire horse fuels energy that can tip into burnout and stress, so there is real potential for health problems if you are not careful. This is especially true if the stars in your health palace are weaker or inauspicious. Pay deliberate attention to it.

## The Practical Takeaway

The fire horse rewards people who prepare and punishes people who drift. Use Purple Star to pinpoint where the year will concentrate, gauge how favorable that area is for you, and shore up the weak spots, with health near the top of the list. Do that, and you are positioned to capture the upside the year offers while avoiding the chaos.
