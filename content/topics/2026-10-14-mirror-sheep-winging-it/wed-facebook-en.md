The Sheep doesn't plan. That's why you have to.

The Sheep isn't into the logic and thinking that goes into planning. It's a free spirit. It wings it. And when a whole year takes on that quality, even careful people feel it. The budget gets pushed. The thing you were going to think over gets decided on a whim.

Money is where that could be most disastrous. The Sheep may not be great at making money, but it's sure good at spending it.

So next year two boring things matter a lot: balancing the budget and watching your cash flow. Get them on paper before February 6, while your head is cool.

Are you a planner or do you wing it? Be honest.
