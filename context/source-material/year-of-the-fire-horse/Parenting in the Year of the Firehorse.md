# Parenting in the Year of the Firehorse

Notes from the June 23, 2026 conversation between Bill and David Hajdu, sparked by Father's Day. A potential blog post, possibly a two-part series.

## The thesis

The year of the firehorse is about passion. A baby born this year should be expected to be very high energy and, frankly, high crying. That means high potential and high risk. The core message for parents: having a baby this year, more than any other, demands that you really focus on being a parent. Sharpen your parenting skills as fast as you can, because it is going to be a wild ride.

Of the 60 Chinese signs (12 animals x 5 elements), firehorse children are probably the ones most likely to bring both the greatest joy and the greatest aggravation.

## The four pillars matter, not just the year

People tend to think only in terms of the birth year. But there are four pillars (year, month, day, and more), and they layer on top of each other. A baby born this month is already a double horse, because we are in a horse month within the horse year. Three days this stretch produce a triple horse: June 13, June 25, and July 7. Triple horse comes with massive potential and massive risk.

The year is the wood (fire) horse year, and we are currently in a wood horse month that runs roughly June 6 to July 7. The day element layers on further (earth horse day, metal horse day, water horse day, and so on), which is where real personalization comes in. It is not enough to say a child is "born in the year of the firehorse."

## The number one challenge: harnessing the energy

The top job for parents is to harness the firehorse child's energy, enthusiasm, and passion. Several pieces to that:

**Teach them to relax.** Make the baby as relaxed and comfortable as possible. Bill draws on ten years as a nanny for two boys, raised in opposite styles. The first was coddled: daily buggy rides before naps, soothed and held the moment he cried. The second was simply laid down and left to cry it out. A firehorse baby will cry up a storm, so if you go the cry-it-out route, be ready, because it may be more than you can handle. Have good soothing techniques ready (calming music, holding, rocking) and start studying tips now if you are not prepared.

**Channel the energy into sports.** The horse is the sign most closely associated with sports. Find what the child likes and let them pursue it. You cannot push, but you can encourage. Horse is also strong for performing arts (acting, singing, music), so support those hobbies too if the interest shows.

## What to expect from a double horse child

Like a cowboy tying up his horse so it does not wander, a double horse child will likely have a short attention span. This may be the student who gets into trouble, lacks self control, and gets called out by teachers. Helping the child concentrate will be a big challenge. Beyond attention span, they struggle to stick with things: a firehorse will go all-in on projects that finish quickly, but will abandon anything that starts to feel too long.

Once the baby becomes mobile, things get hectic. Bill's wife Tan, doing nanny work, notes that the first month or two are relatively easy, but once the baby starts crawling and moving, a firehorse will be everywhere, fast, and will probably walk early. Baby-proof the house thoroughly.

## Feng Shui is part of this

Bill stresses that in Chinese astrology, feng shui is not separate. Chinese astrology answers questions about time and questions about space, and feng shui is the space side. No important building goes up in Hong Kong without a feng shui master's detailed analysis. If there is ever a time to apply feng shui to your home, it is now. For a firehorse baby, make the environment as soothing as possible: calming colors, color schemes, room layout, everything tuned to help the baby relax.

## One positive note on this month

This is a wood month, and wood is the most positive energy for fire. So a baby born this month has slightly more big positives than big negatives. The odds tilt just a little in the baby's favor.

## Months ahead: what amplifies and what balances

The overall framing is to talk about which months **amplify** the firehorse behavior and which **balance** it, rather than labeling months "good" or "bad."

**Amplifies, #1: Wood Horse (current month).** A child born here leans hardest into the firehorse traits.

**Amplifies, #2: Wood Goat / Sheep (next month).** Elements come two months in a row, so next month is also a wood month. Sheep is the soulmate sign for horse, which amplifies the likelihood of something important and meaningful early on. Sheep and horse are highly compatible, both fire signs. Sheep is the yin to horse's yang, a gentler fire that can calm the baby somewhat. But sheep is more emotional than horse, so the baby will need even more attention, stroking, and emotional support. The surrounding energy calms a bit, yet parenting may actually get harder because you must focus heavily on the baby's emotions.

**Balances: Dog month (Metal Dog, roughly October 8 to November 6).** Dog is a much more relaxed month and complements the horse, encouraging it to slow down. With a metal year and a fire horse, fire controls metal, which makes this a calming situation for the baby.

## The big takeaway

Parents thinking about a firehorse child should dive into all four pillars, not just the year, because each one shades the next. And the deeper lesson mirrors good coaching: you do not learn one technique and apply it to everyone. You understand how each child is different. The cosmic energy around each person varies based on many specifics, not just a birth year.

A practical sign to watch: if a firehorse baby is lying around and not very active, do not just count your blessings. Ask why the firehorse baby is not raising hell, because that is what they normally do. Knowing the astrological makeup helps you read when the baby is in their comfort zone and when they are not. A hyperactive firehorse baby is not cause for worry. It is just a matter of helping them channel the energy, and creating situations where the baby chooses to relax. It is all about customization. You cannot treat every baby the same.
