## FAQ Schema

**Q: How do you practice Wu Wei in daily life?**
A: Start with one specific thing, not a broad review of your life. Find the place where you are working harder than the results justify. Ask whether your effort is aligned with the actual current of the situation or working against it. Then, rather than adding more effort, experiment with doing less in that one area, and notice what opens up when the forcing stops.

**Q: What does "find your curl" mean in the context of Wu Wei?**
A: The curl refers to the inside of a wave, where a skilled surfer positions themselves so the wave's own energy does the work. "Find your curl" means identify the natural current of a situation and position your action within it, rather than fighting the wave from the outside. It is the practical question Wu Wei asks of any challenge.

**Q: What is the Wu Wei question to ask yourself?**
A: "Am I working against the current of this situation, or with it?" Not whether you are working hard enough or committed enough, but whether your approach is aligned with what the situation actually needs right now. The flailing surfer is committed and working hard. The question is whether that effort is in the right direction.

**Q: How long should you try the Wu Wei practice before expecting results?**
A: The immediate shift is not in results but in perspective. When you stop forcing a situation, even for a weekend, you often see it differently. New information arrives. The other person in a conversation does something unexpected because you have stopped filling all the space. The practice creates conditions; it does not manufacture outcomes. Start with one weekend.

**Q: What is Bill Hajdu's Tao Te Ching book structure?**
A: The retranslation is structured with the verse translation on the left-hand page and three to five practical applications on the right-hand page, applicable on almost any ordinary day. The goal is to make Taoist wisdom immediately usable, not just admirable. The standard is clear enough language to survive Google Translation into any other language without losing the original idea.
