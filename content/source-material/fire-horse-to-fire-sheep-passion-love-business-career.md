# From Fire Horse to Fire Sheep: Where the Passion Goes (Love, Business, Career)

**Source:** Bill × Dave recording, 2026-09-28 (Mahjong Mirror interview, first ~25 minutes; the rest of the call is memoir material for Bill's book, not astrology)
**Now:** about a quarter of the Fire Horse year left
**Next year:** the Fire Sheep, begins Feb 6, 2027 (see `year-of-the-sheep-2027.md`)
**Post angle:** Two fire years back to back. The passion carries over; its direction flips. The Horse's passion breaks things, the Sheep's passion builds them. Thread it through three areas: love, business, career.

## The thread: same fire, opposite direction
- The Sheep is the Yin to the Horse's Yang. The Horse is the male sign, the Sheep the female sign.
- 2026 and 2027 are both fire years, and the Horse and the Sheep are both fire signs. Next year is a **double fire year** again, so passion stays high.
- The difference is what the passion does. The Horse is about **independence, a little adventure, and leaving** things that no longer bring satisfaction: it will soon leave a situation it isn't getting satisfaction from. The Sheep is about **making, fostering, staying in and strengthening relationships**. "Relationships are important to the Sheep. I don't want to say they're exactly needy or clingy, but you get the idea. It's the exact opposite of the horse."
- So the Horse's passion is anti-relationship; the Sheep's is pro-relationship. This year may be better for making money than for relationships. Next year flips: better odds for relationships, worse odds in business.

## Love and romance
- **This year (Fire Horse):** passion tends to show up negatively: arguments, breakups, stress, not getting along. A few relationships will be absolutely fantastic; the majority will struggle. Relationships are more likely to break up "because of too much passion." The flow favors arguments and even broken relationships.
- **Next year (Fire Sheep):** the passion goes to deeper love and deeper relationships. "I'm not saying there won't be heated arguments, but the flow favors deepening relationships." The Sheep is more about love and romance than about breaking up, so next year's passion is likely to be far more constructive than this year's.
- **If you're in a relationship:** it's time to do your best to move it up a level. "Love could be in the air next year." Dating to engaged, engaged to married: next year is a good year to work on it and give it your all.
- **If you're single:** "the worst thing you can do is sit on the sidelines." Get out there, make yourself available, meet people. There should be good opportunities, but it takes effort: "nothing comes automatic, even when the flow is in your favor."
- Overall: put it all out there in love next year; it's a good time to take relationships to the next level.

## Business and money
- In both Mahjong and astrology, the Sheep is associated with **spending, especially overspending**. (Bill, aside: "she spends money.")
- Be careful with business decisions: don't stretch yourself too thin, don't get carried away by emotions, don't over-extend, don't let passion push you into reckless decisions.
- Bill's example: even in AI there are warnings the investment bubble may be getting ready to break. "I'm not saying you need to be scared, but you need to keep self control."
- A double fire year pulls toward taking risks; in a Sheep year that may not be a great idea. Be conservative rather than high-risk. Stick with whatever business plan you have.
- **One word for business: caution.**

## Career (most readers have a career, not a business)
- The Fire Sheep doesn't favor relationships at work the way it favors them in love, but it's still a good sign for relationships, and relationships at work will matter a lot next year **because people are going to be sensitive.**
- If your passion leads you into conflict with people at work, it will hurt your career. Keep on good terms; put positive passion into the relationships that count at work.
- **Whenever you find yourself in conflict with somebody at work, the best thing is to walk away.** Don't lose your self-control and say or do things you'll regret.
- Above all, avoid a personality conflict with the boss "at all costs."
- **Passion is a double-edged sword for career.** Channel the excess fire into motivation and hard work and you can do well; lose control and it can hurt your chances. "It could be the best of years or the worst of years for career. It depends on how you channel your passion and keep your emotions under control."
- Keeping your emotions under control is "probably the No. 1 thing you have to do in a Sheep year, especially in a double fire Sheep year: self control."
