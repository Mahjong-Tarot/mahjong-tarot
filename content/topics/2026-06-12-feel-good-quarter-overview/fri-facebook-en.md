Feel Good Friday: stop swimming against the month.

This week we mapped the quarter: the Dragon was for action, the Snake is for depth, the Water Horse opens June 15 and wants prepared boldness.

Your weekend challenge: map the next six weeks on paper. Just fifteen minutes. Then pick two things.

One bold move you'll make when the Water Horse opens. Not a vague hope. A specific, named decision or action you're committing to.

One thing you'll hold. The move that belongs in a different window. Name it. Give it a date to revisit: June 15 or later.

That's it. Plan with the current, not against it.

The people who come out of a hard quarter strong are not the ones who pushed hardest every day. They're the ones who knew when to push and when to hold.

You have the map. What are your two things? Drop them in the comments.
