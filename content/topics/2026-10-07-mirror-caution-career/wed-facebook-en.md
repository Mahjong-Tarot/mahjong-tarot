One word for your money in the Fire Sheep year: caution.

On Monday I told you the good news: next year's fire turns toward love. Here's the other half. The same flip works against your wallet.

In Mahjong as well as Chinese astrology, the Sheep is associated with spending, especially overspending. And a double fire year pulls you toward taking risks. An animal that likes to spend, on a year that likes to gamble.

So: don't stretch yourself too thin. Don't let emotion make the call. Stick with the plan you already have.

Not scared. Careful. There's a difference.

Are you a planner or an impulse spender? Be honest.
