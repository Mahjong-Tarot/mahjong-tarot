## FAQ Schema

**Q: Is the Metal Snake month bad for everyone in 2026?**
A: The Metal Snake month (May 17–Jun 14, 2026) is generally inauspicious in the Fire Horse year because fire destroys metal's luck. The Rat has the hardest time. The Ox and Rooster are exceptions. The Snake month is actually one of their better stretches of the year.

**Q: What are the luckiest days in the Snake month 2026?**
A: Water days are the most favorable. The best days are Sheep days (especially water Sheep days) because the Sheep is compatible with both the Snake and the Horse. Rooster days are also positive. Avoid Rat days.

**Q: What should I avoid during the Metal Snake month?**
A: Avoid starting new ventures or projects, taking financial risks or aggressive bets, and any form of deception or dishonesty. Getting caught in even a small lie this month can cause disproportionate relationship damage in a high-fire year.

**Q: What is the Metal Snake month good for?**
A: Nurturing existing relationships, intellectual and mental work (rather than speculative risk), rest and recovery, and consolidating what you built during the Dragon month. It is a depth month, not a chasing month.

**Q: Why does the Fire Horse year make the Snake month harder?**
A: The year is fire energy; the month is metal energy. Fire destroys metal in the Chinese five-elements cycle, which means metal's traditional strengths (precision, structure, steady gains) are working against the year's grain. Add that the Snake and Horse animals are naturally incompatible, and the month carries double friction.
