The most auspicious month of the year is also the most emotionally demanding one to parent through. Nobody tells new parents that part.

Two weeks ago I wrote about the "double horse" baby -- born in the wild window before July 7, high energy, high crying, high everything. Parents whose babies are arriving now, in what Chinese astrology calls the Soulmate Month, have understandably assumed they got the calmer draw. They're half right.

The Sheep is the Horse's soulmate sign, and the month's wood element feeds the year's fire element -- genuinely one of the most favorable combinations in the whole system. The source material is direct about it: expect something important and meaningful, early on, for a baby born in this window.

Here's the part that gets missed. The Sheep is also the sign of emotions. The room gets calmer. The parenting gets harder, because this baby isn't asking for less of you -- it's asking for a different kind of attention: presence, emotional responsiveness, patience with a child who reads your mood before you've said a word.

The pattern holds beyond parenting too: the version of a challenge that looks easiest on the surface is often the one that asks the most of you in a currency you weren't budgeting for.
