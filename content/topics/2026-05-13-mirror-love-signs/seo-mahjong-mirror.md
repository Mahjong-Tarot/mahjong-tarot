## FAQ Schema
**Q: What is the Third Angle of the Mahjong Mirror?**
A: The Third Angle examines opposition: the obstacles and challenges blocking you from your goal. In love, it reveals the structural forces underneath surface arguments: unspoken expectations, inertia, competing core themes, or emotional distance that has accumulated gradually.

**Q: What does the Peach Blossom tile mean in a Mahjong reading?**
A: The Peach Blossom tile represents romantic temptation and affairs. When it appears in the opposition position of a reading, it indicates that romantic distraction (an attraction pulling energy away from the primary relationship) is the blocking force.

**Q: What does the Sword tile mean in a love reading?**
A: The Sword tile in the opposition position signals that clarity is needed. It's not necessarily about ending a relationship. It often means ending a pattern or a story you've been telling yourself. The Sword says: stop circling. Decide.

**Q: Can I use the Mahjong Mirror framework without a reading?**
A: Yes. The Third Angle framework can be applied directly using three questions: What are we not saying to each other? Where am I part of the opposition? What am I actually afraid of here? The book walks through this process in Section 3.

**Q: Why does the Fire Horse year make relational opposition worse?**
A: The Fire Horse is an amplifier. Whatever has been accumulating in a relationship (unresolved tension, emotional distance, unspoken needs) surfaces faster and more forcefully in 2026 than it would in a quieter year. The year does not coexist peacefully with stagnation.
