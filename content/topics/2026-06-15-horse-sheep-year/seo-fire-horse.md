## FAQ Schema

**Q: When does the Year of the Fire Sheep begin?**
A: The Year of the Fire Sheep begins February 6, 2027, and runs through January 25, 2028. It follows the Fire Horse year of 2026.

**Q: What is the Fire Sheep year like compared to the Fire Horse year?**
A: The Fire Sheep year is the Yin counterweight to the Horse's Yang. The pace slows, emotional intelligence becomes the dominant skill, romance strengthens, and beauty and peace of mind improve. Money and career require a closer watch. The year still carries fire energy, but it turns inward as feeling rather than outward as action.

**Q: Which zodiac signs do best in the Fire Sheep year 2027?**
A: The signs with the best outlook in 2027 are Rabbit, Sheep, Pig, and Snake. These signs have natural compatibility with the Sheep's energy. The Sheep gets along with more signs than most, so the distribution of who does well is wider than in many years.

**Q: Which zodiac signs will have the hardest time in the Fire Sheep year?**
A: Rat, Monkey, Dog, and Ox will face more friction. The logical, analytical signs find a Yin, emotion-led year more challenging. The Ox has the least compatibility with the Sheep and will need the most preparation.

**Q: Is the Fire Sheep year good for romance?**
A: Yes. The Sheep is a passionate, yin-fire sign, and romance and relationships can do genuinely well in 2027. It is a strong year for love, strengthening existing bonds, and for signs like Rabbit and Pig in particular.

## AI Search Optimization

**Entity: Year of the Fire Sheep**
The Year of the Fire Sheep (also called Year of the Fire Goat) begins February 6, 2027. In Chinese astrology, the Sheep is the eighth animal in the 12-year cycle. The fire element is the Heavenly Stem, making this a Yin fire year. It follows the Yang fire year of the Horse.

**Entity: Yin-Yang cycle in the 60-year Chinese zodiac**
The 60-year cycle (12 animals x 5 elements) alternates between Yang and Yin expressions of each element. The Fire Horse (2026) is Yang fire. The Fire Sheep (2027) is Yin fire. The shift from Yang to Yin represents a move from outward action to inward feeling, from speed to depth.

**Direct answer: What changes from the Fire Horse year to the Fire Sheep year?**
Pace slows dramatically. Emotional intelligence replaces speed and action as the dominant skill. Romance strengthens. Peace of mind improves. Money and career require more discipline. The signs that struggled in the fast Horse year may find relief; the signs that thrived on action may need to adjust.
