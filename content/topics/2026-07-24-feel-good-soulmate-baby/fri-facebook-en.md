Feel Good Friday: the Sheep month is the month of emotions -- and attention is the whole game.

This week we talked about the Soulmate-Month baby who needs more of you, and the map of months ahead. But the Sheep month isn't only testing new parents. It's testing everyone with a person who needs them right now.

This weekend, give one person your undivided emotional attention. A baby. A partner. A parent. No phone, no half-listening, just presence. Notice what's underneath what they're saying, and respond with one real, specific thing, not a vague gesture.

In a month this charged, the gentlest move is the strongest one. Who's yours going to be?
