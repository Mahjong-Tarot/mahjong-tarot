I've called myself a Taoist for 30 years, and in the middle of the most frantic year in 60, I keep coming back to one idea from the Tao Te Ching: Wu Wei.

People think it means "go with the flow." It doesn't. It means effortless action: doing by not doing.

Picture the best surfer on the North Shore, barely moving inside the wave while everyone else wipes out. That champion is not working less. He is working in perfect alignment with the current. The wave does the work. He reads it and moves with it. The flailing surfer next to him works ten times as hard and falls in every time.

Most people in the Fire Horse year are the flailing surfer. Trying harder. Moving faster. Forcing decisions. And getting thrown off balance by a year that isn't asking for more force. It's asking for better alignment.

That's what 2,500 years of Taoist wisdom has to say about 2026.

Where in your life are you flailing right now instead of riding the curl?
