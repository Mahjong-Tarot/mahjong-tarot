Single? Here's my advice for 2027: the worst thing you can do is sit on the sidelines.

The Fire Sheep year favors love. The passion of this year's fire carries over, but next year it points toward making and strengthening relationships instead of leaving them. If you're single, you should have some good opportunities.

But I'll tell you what I tell everyone in a reading: nothing comes automatic, even when the flow is in your favor. The Sheep opens the door. You still have to walk through it.

So if the Horse year left you burned and you've been telling yourself you're done with all of it, be done with the Horse year, not with love. Get out there. Make yourself available. Meet people.

Who's ready to stop sitting this one out?
