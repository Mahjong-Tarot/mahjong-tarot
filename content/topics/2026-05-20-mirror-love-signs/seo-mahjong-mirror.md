## FAQ Schema
**Q: What does the Peach Blossom tile mean in a love reading?**
A: The Peach Blossom tile represents romantic temptation and the pull of new attraction. When it appears in a love reading's opposition position, it points to an unmet need in the current relationship. Not necessarily a literal affair, but a hunger for passion, pursuit, or being truly seen that the relationship isn't currently providing.

**Q: What does the Sword tile mean in a love reading?**
A: The Sword tile indicates something that needs to end or be cut cleanly. In a love reading, it can point to the end of the relationship itself or, more commonly, to a pattern or dynamic within the relationship that has been running too long and needs to stop. It gives permission to name what both people already know.

**Q: What is the Third Angle of the Mahjong Mirror?**
A: The Third Angle of the Mahjong Mirror asks: what is opposing you? In love, it examines the structural obstacle beneath the surface arguments, not what you're fighting about but what would still be in the way even if that fight stopped. It is the angle most people resist because it requires honesty about something they've been avoiding.

**Q: Why are the Peach Blossom and Sword tiles appearing more often in Fire Horse year readings?**
A: The Fire Horse year amplifies suppressed dynamics and accelerates what was already in motion. Unmet needs become harder to ignore. Patterns that weren't working become impossible to sustain. The Peach Blossom and Sword are the tiles that surface what the Fire Horse year's pressure is exposing.

**Q: How do I use the Third Angle of the Mahjong Mirror in my relationship?**
A: Start with three questions: What is the unmet need in my relationship I haven't said out loud? What pattern keeps repeating no matter who the other person is? What would I cut if I were being fully honest? These questions constitute the Third Angle applied to love without a formal reading.
