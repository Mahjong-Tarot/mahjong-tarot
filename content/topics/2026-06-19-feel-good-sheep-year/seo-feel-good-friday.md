**Q: Why should I prepare for the Year of the Sheep 2027 now?**
A: The Fire Horse year is still running through February 5, 2027. Yang energy, decisive and action-oriented, is available until the last day. Yin years like the Sheep reward what you built in the preceding Yang year, not what you assemble after the new year begins. Using the Horse year's remaining momentum to build toward what you want in the Sheep year is more effective than starting fresh in a slower, more emotionally complex energy.

**Q: What are the best things to build before the Year of the Sheep 2027?**
A: Emotional steadiness (a practice for managing your own feelings and reading others'); a key relationship tended with intention now; a financial discipline that holds through a year inclined toward spending; or a personal practice (morning routine, creative habit, physical discipline) that anchors you when the emotional energy of the Sheep year runs high.

**Q: How is the Feel Good Friday challenge different from a New Year's resolution?**
A: A New Year's resolution waits until the calendar turns and starts in the new energy. This challenge uses the remaining Horse year energy, which is decisive and action-oriented, to start building before the Yin year begins. One specific, nameable thing, started this weekend, built over months, carried forward on February 6, 2027.
