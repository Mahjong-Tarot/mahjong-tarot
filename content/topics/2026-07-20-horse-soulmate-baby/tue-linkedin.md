A quick follow-up on yesterday's point, because the mechanic behind it is worth understanding on its own.

In Chinese astrology, the Sheep and the Horse are soulmate signs -- the highest compatibility rating a month sign can carry with a year sign. Layer on the element interplay (the month is wood, the year is fire, and wood feeds fire) and you get one of the most favorable combinations the system recognizes. For a baby born in this window, that's not abstract. It means the conditions are genuinely stacked in their favor from the start.

What it doesn't mean is less work for the parents. The Sheep is the sign of emotions, so the demand simply shifts from managing energy to meeting emotional need -- showing up consistently, reading cues, being present in a way that has nothing to do with keeping up physically.

The lesson generalizes past parenting: favorable conditions rarely mean less effort. They usually mean the effort moves somewhere less visible.
