## FAQ Schema
**Q: How do I channel a firehorse baby's energy?**
A: The horse is the sign of sports and performing arts. Watch for what physical activities or creative pursuits light your child up, and make space for those rather than trying to suppress the energy. You cannot push a child toward an activity, but you can encourage the one they love.

**Q: How do I create a calming space for a firehorse baby using feng shui?**
A: Choose one room or corner and tune it toward calm: soothing colors (avoid very bright reds or oranges), clean and minimal layout, soft warm lighting rather than harsh overhead light, and a soft surface for the child to rest on. The goal is a space the environment signals as a place to slow down -- so the child can choose to relax there.

**Q: What soothing techniques work for high-energy firehorse babies?**
A: Build your toolkit in the quieter moments, not the crisis ones. Try calming music specific to what works for your baby, holding, rocking, and swaddling. Note that the cry-it-out approach with a firehorse baby tends to produce longer and louder crying than with calmer signs -- be prepared before attempting it.

**Q: How does the Wood Sheep month (July 7 to August 7) affect a firehorse baby?**
A: The Sheep is the soulmate sign for the Horse: both fire signs, highly compatible, but the Sheep is the yin (calmer, more emotional). The surrounding environment may feel slightly less frenetic than the Wood Horse month. However, the Sheep's emotional nature means the baby may need more emotional attention -- more holding, more response to feelings -- even if the raw physical energy dials down slightly.

**Q: What is the five-pillar challenge for firehorse parents?**
A: The practical challenge is to look at the full four pillars of your child's chart (year, month, day, and hour of birth), not just the birth year. A Water day cools firehorse energy significantly; an Earth day grounds it; another Horse day doubles it. Knowing the full chart helps you see your child more clearly and adjust your parenting approach to the specific individual.
