Next year is another fire year, but it's a different fire.

The Horse carries Yang fire: fire out of control. The Sheep carries Yin fire: controlled fire. There's still plenty of passion. You just get to decide where it goes.

Here's how I'd use it. In a conflict, seek resolution, because the Sheep is a peaceful sign and the odds are in your favor. In money, go slow. Make sure you know what you're doing and aim for stability. In love, it may be all right to throw caution to the wind every now and then.

Three parts of your life. Three different speeds.

Which one do you need to slow down?
