The Snake month is a relationship month, not a risk month.

In Chinese astrology, some months are built for launching and some are built for tending. The Snake month is a tending month. It does not favor the new. It favors the existing: the relationship you already have, the work you've already invested in, the understanding that's been slowly building between you and someone you care about.

The Mahjong Mirror's second angle asks: who are you in this situation? Not who you want to be eventually, or who you were before. Who are you right now, in this specific relationship, with this specific person?

The discipline of the Snake month is to go deeper rather than wider. One relationship that gets your real attention this month will be stronger on the other side. One honest conversation you've been postponing will clear more air than three new connections could fill.

That's the Mirror's move for the Snake month: go deep on what you already have.
