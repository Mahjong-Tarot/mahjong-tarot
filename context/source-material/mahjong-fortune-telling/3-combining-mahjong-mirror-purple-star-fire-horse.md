# Combining the Mahjong Mirror, Purple Star, and the Year of the Fire Horse

Three tools work together here. The Mahjong Mirror is the decision making framework. Purple Star is the astrological tool. The year of the fire horse is the timing and context. You run all of it through the Mahjong Mirror, and the more perspectives you bring, the better prepared and more informed you are.

## Why You Combine Them

An astrology reading on its own is great. A card reading on its own is great. Working through the decision making framework in the Mahjong Mirror book on its own is great. But you can never have too many perspectives when something in your life matters. Putting the three together makes you more effective, more informed, and more prepared to make good use of a year as intense as the fire horse.

The Mahjong Mirror emphasizes holding up the mirror to yourself across all 12 areas of your life. In a fast year, it is easy to fixate on one area and neglect others. Looking honestly at each palace, and pairing that self reflection with the data from a reading, closes the gap.

## How Purple Star Strengthens a Card Reading

There are at least three or four solid reasons to run a Purple Star reading alongside a card reading.

Confirmation comes first. In a recent reading the cards showed several peach cards and the Purple Star chart showed the Peach Blossom Star, and both pointed to the same thing in the same palace. When two independent methods agree, you can trust the signal.

Added information comes next. Each method can surface something the other misses, which is the whole point of stacking perspectives.

Specificity is the third. A card reading does not tell you how auspicious each area of your life is. If a card says "get some help," it may not say from whom, perhaps only "a young girl." Is that a young friend, relative, or coworker? Your Purple Star palaces show which relationships are more auspicious, so the chart sharpens a vague card into a concrete direction.

## The Consultation: Why This Was Hard Before, and Why AI Changes It

In the old days, combining these methodologies was extremely hard. Pulling together all the data and holding it in one place was not possible in a person's head, and was even beyond older computers. With AI, all the needed data can be assembled so the astrologer spends their time on what humans do best: observing, interpreting, and advising. It is also more accurate because the data is pulled without mistakes.

That is the value of a consultation. The technology gathers and organizes everything, and the astrologer delivers real interpretation on top of clean, complete data, combining the Mahjong Mirror framework, the Purple Star chart, and the realities of the fire horse year into one informed reading.
