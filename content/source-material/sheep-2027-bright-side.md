# The Bright Side of the Sheep (2027, the Fire Sheep)

**Source:** Bill × Dave recording, 2026-10-12 (Mahjong Mirror interview, 01:05 to 10:55). Lark Minutes: https://edge8company.sg.larksuite.com/minutes/obsgkmx7hk7a954gz6p8egqc
**Begins:** February 6, 2027
**Post angle:** Bill felt guilty for only covering the downside, so this is the positive. Two big things to know: the Sheep is the eighth sign, and it is the strongest Yin sign.

## Eight and prosperity
- The Sheep is the eighth sign in Chinese astrology. The number 8 in Chinese thought is a positive sign for prosperity and good luck.

## Yin, and how you win next year
- The Sheep is a Yin sign, the strongest Chinese sign exhibiting feminine qualities.
- 2026 is a Horse year: the way to behave is the aggressive, Yang, macho approach. Next year the approach that gains prosperity is Yin, almost a Taoist approach. You succeed by yielding. Indirect approach. Taking your time.
- "The Sheep can lose and lose and lose and lose and lose an argument, and all of a sudden the Sheep wins." That style is much more likely to succeed than charging right in.

## What does well
- Beauty, romance, creativity, the arts, gentleness. Anything to do with these has a great chance of succeeding next year. The beauty and cosmetics industry. Romance.
- **Love:** normally a great year for romance. In a relationship, a great time to move it to the next level if that's what you're thinking about. Not in one, you should be out there working on it. One of the good years for romance to flare up.
- **Caution as a virtue:** Sheep are cautious, in love and business. It may be all right to throw caution to the wind now and then in romance, but in money, follow the Sheep's inclination: take it easy, go slow, make sure you know what you are doing, aim for stability and conservative investments.
- **Peace:** the Sheep is very much a peaceful sign, so it is a good year for conflict resolution. If you're at odds with somebody, this might be a good year to seek resolution. With Sheep energy, the odds are in your favor.
- Bill is looking forward to it. The Sheep is one of the great signs for the Pig (Bill is the Firepig).

## Yin fire
- This is Yin fire: controlled fire. Not the Horse year's Yang fire, which is fire out of control. Controlled fire means a lot of passion in relationships. Look for your love life heating up and make it work for you.
- Emotions run strong, so even though the Sheep likes peace there could be conflict and flare-ups when emotions get out of control. Control your emotions in any fire year.
- More passion, for good and for difficulties. Maybe a little more risk-taking than a Sheep normally would. Sheep might be a little less cautious this year.

## The best signs (this supersedes the May list)
- The three best animal signs: **Rabbit (the Cat in Vietnam), Pig and Horse.**
- Elements are just as important as animal signs. Ideal is one of those signs with an **Earth or Wood** birth element.
- Wood with fire results in a lot of positive energy, which helps get things done and achieve goals.
- Fire with earth produces metal, which is good for making money. An **Earth Pig or Earth Rabbit** in the Fire Sheep year could be really good for financial success.
- The Horse too, but not the Fire Horse: fire plus Fire Sheep is too much. It would have to be an Earth Horse or a Wood Horse.
