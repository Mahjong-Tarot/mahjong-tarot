Feel Good Friday: a hyperactive firehorse kid is not a problem. It is potential pointed in every direction at once.

The horse is the sign of sports and performing arts. That is not a coincidence. The fire in them needs to go somewhere, and the best outcomes come when you find the direction rather than trying to slow the engine down.

Two moves for this weekend:

One: find one outlet for the energy. Watch what your child runs toward naturally -- the physical activity, the creative pursuit, the thing that holds their attention in a way nothing else does. That is the direction. Make more space for it.

Two: build one pocket of calm. One corner of the home, tuned for quiet. Calming colors, soft light, a comfortable place to land. Not a place they are sent to -- a place they choose. That is feng shui in practice, and it is one of the most practical parenting moves you can make this year.

The fire is not the problem. The direction is the question.
