## FAQ Schema

**Q: What does "planning with the current" mean in Chinese astrology?**
A: It means aligning your biggest efforts with the months that support those types of efforts. Bold moves in months with opportunity energy (like the Dragon month). Consolidation in months that reward depth and patience (like the Snake month). Fast action when acceleration months open (like the Water Horse). Working against this rhythm causes unnecessary exhaustion.

**Q: What should I do before the Water Horse month opens on June 15?**
A: Map the next six weeks: understand what the Snake month (through June 14) is asking for (protection, depth, honesty) and what the Water Horse month will want (prepared, specific, bold action). Name one bold move you'll make when the Water Horse opens. Name one thing you'll hold during the remaining Snake month days.

**Q: Is Chinese astrology about giving up control to the stars?**
A: No. Working with the year's energy is a discipline of honest self-assessment: knowing where you're spending effort and whether the timing supports it. The Fire Horse year rewards people who make intentional choices about when to push and when to hold.

**Q: What is the Feel Good Friday series?**
A: Feel Good Friday is a weekly content series from Bill Hajdu at The Mahjong Tarot. Every Friday post ties the week's Fire Horse and Mahjong Mirror themes together with an actionable weekend challenge: uplifting, practical, and grounded in the year's energy.
