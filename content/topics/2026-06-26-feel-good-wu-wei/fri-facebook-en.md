Feel Good Friday: Find your curl.

This weekend, I want you to try one thing from the right-hand page of the Tao Te Ching book I'm working on.

Pick the single place in your life right now where you are clearly working harder than the results justify. You already know which one it is. The thing that came to mind before I finished that sentence.

Then ask the Wu Wei question: am I working against the current of this situation, or with it?

And then, just for this weekend, do less with it. Not nothing. Less. Stop forcing the conversation. Step back from the project that's stuck. Put down the worry you've been gripping all week.

The champion surfer in the curl isn't doing more than everyone else. He's doing exactly what the wave needs, at the moment it needs it. The rest is the wave.

Most people respond to hard problems by adding more force. Wu Wei says: before you add, check your alignment.

What's your one place this weekend? Drop it in the comments if you want.
