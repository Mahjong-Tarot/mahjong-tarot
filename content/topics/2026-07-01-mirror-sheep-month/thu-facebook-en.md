The Mirror's discipline for a passionate month: before the big conversation, empty the cup.

Most arguments in a two-fire month aren't about what they seem to be about. They're about pace and pride. One person moves too fast, the other feels run over. One person says something sharp because the temperature in the room made it feel easy, even satisfying. And then a moment that could have gone somewhere real goes somewhere painful instead.

Before the big talk this month: sit down with the Second Angle first. Who are you right now, in this relationship? Be specific. Be honest. Not with me. With yourself.

Then go in knowing what you actually want. Not what you want to win. What you want the relationship to look like.

The month will hand you openings. What you do with them is on you.
