# Thursday Facebook (EN) - May 14

Two tiles. Two completely different messages.

The Peach Blossom in the opposition position of a reading tells me the blocking force is temptation: an attraction pulling energy away from where it belongs.

The Sword in the opposition position tells me something else: a decision is overdue. Not necessarily the end of the relationship. The end of a pattern. The end of a story that isn't true anymore.

Both tiles are appearing in readings this year more than I'd expect. The Fire Horse doesn't create these situations. It just makes the ones already present impossible to keep hiding.
