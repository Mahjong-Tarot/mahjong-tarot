## FAQ Schema

**Q: What is the Mahjong Mirror's four-angle framework?**
A: The Mahjong Mirror uses four angles to analyze any significant decision or situation: the central theme (what's actually at stake), Know Thyself (who you are in this situation), the opposition (what's working against you), and the future (what you're building toward). It uses Mahjong tiles to surface honest answers.

**Q: How do I know when to push and when to hold in the Fire Horse year?**
A: Apply the Mirror's first angle, "what is this month actually for?", to each month. The Metal Dragon (Apr 17–May 16) was for bold moves. The Metal Snake (May 17–Jun 14) is for consolidation. The Water Horse (Jun 15+) is for acceleration in the direction you've built. Each month has a different mandate.

**Q: What is the most important question before the Water Horse month opens?**
A: The Mirror's third angle asks: what are you still carrying from the Snake month that needs resolution before June 15? Unresolved friction from the Snake month doesn't disappear in the Water Horse. It accelerates.

**Q: How is the Mahjong Mirror different from a regular horoscope?**
A: The Mahjong Mirror is a decision-making framework, not a horoscope. It doesn't predict outcomes; it asks questions that help you see your situation more clearly. It can be applied to any decision, any month, any situation, without a card reading, using the book alone.
