A teapot has to be emptied before it can be refilled.

That's the Tao. That's also the Mirror.

Lao Tzu wasn't writing for philosophers. He was writing for people who use teapots. The image is that simple. And that useful.

A full cup can't receive anything new. Not more tea. Not a better perspective. Not an honest read of what's actually happening. First it has to be emptied.

The Mahjong Mirror starts there. Before you decide, before you act, before you plan: empty the cup.

That's Wu Wei. Not emptiness for its own sake. The emptiness that makes the next thing possible.
