Feel Good Friday: three words for the Snake month.

Rest. Caution. Honesty.

This week we looked at why the Metal Snake month is the Fire Horse year's toughest stretch, and three Mirror moves to navigate it productively. Today I want to make it even simpler.

Your weekend challenge is one of each:

Schedule real downtime. Not productive rest. Not scrolling on the couch. Whatever actually restores you: a walk without headphones, slow cooking, a long conversation with someone you've been meaning to really talk to. Name it. Schedule it.

Pause one risky impulse. Not everything on your plate. Just the one move that's going faster than it should this month. Put it in a folder labeled "open after June 14." You lose nothing. You gain a better window.

Have the honest conversation you've been putting off. Not the whole backlog. Just the one that's been sitting there the longest. Pick it. Have it this weekend.

Three small things. A weekend that works with the Snake month instead of against it.
