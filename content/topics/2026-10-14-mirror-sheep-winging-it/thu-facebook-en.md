You can hand someone a perfect spreadsheet in a Sheep year and it will just sit there. So here's a plan that doesn't feel like one.

Next time you're tempted to spend on something you didn't plan, ask the four questions of the Mahjong Mirror:

What is actually at stake here?
Who am I in this decision, calm or carried away?
What's working against me?
What does this build toward?

A question gets through when a spreadsheet won't. And next year, what's working against you is often the flow of the year itself, telling you it'll all work out.

Which of the four would stop you the fastest?
