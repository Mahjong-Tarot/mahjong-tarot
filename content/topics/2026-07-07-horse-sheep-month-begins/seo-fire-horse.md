## FAQ Schema

**Q: When does the Wood Sheep month 2026 start?**
A: The Wood Sheep month 2026 begins on July 7, 2026 at the Xiaoshu solar term, and runs to approximately August 7, 2026.

**Q: What is the Soulmate Month?**
A: The Soulmate Month occurs when the Chinese month sign and the year sign are soulmates. In 2026, the Wood Sheep month is the Soulmate Month inside the Fire Horse year. Major life events, especially in love and relationships, tend to cluster in this window more than at any other time of the year.

**Q: What is the one rule for the Wood Sheep month 2026?**
A: Lead with self-control. Two fire signs together (Sheep and Horse) create high passion and high tempers. Caution says hold back; self-control says move with intention rather than with heat. The month rewards people who decided what they wanted before the passion of the month made decisions for them.

**Q: Which signs are favored in the Wood Sheep month 2026?**
A: About half the signs are favored: Horse, Pig, Rabbit, Dragon, Dog, and Snake. The other half (Rat, Ox, Tiger, Monkey, Rooster) need to focus on tending existing relationships and avoiding unnecessary conflicts.
