Here's what tests people most about loving a Sheep.

Even when they know exactly what they're doing, even when they're perfectly competent, they still want to lean on you.

You'll be tempted to say, "You don't need me for this." You're right. They don't. They want you anyway.

So if someone calls this weekend to talk through a decision they've already made, try not fixing it. Lend the shoulder. It costs you five minutes, and it's probably what the whole conversation was about.

And yes, they can be draining. You're allowed to notice. Loving a Sheep doesn't mean you can never go for a walk.
