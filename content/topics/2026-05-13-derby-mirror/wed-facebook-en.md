Bill wrote the Mahjong Mirror partly because of decisions like the ones the favorites' trainers made at this Derby.

Every trainer had the same information. They all saw the fast pace developing. None of them asked the one question that would have changed everything: what does this pace do to us in the final stretch?

That question is the Third Angle of the Mahjong Mirror. Not "what is the plan?" The plan was solid. Not "what do we want?" They wanted to win. The question is: what is already in front of us that could stop the plan from working?

At the Derby, the obstacle was the fast pace. Golden Tempo's team named it. They built their entire strategy around it. The other trainers ran with confidence and talent and left the obstacle unnamed.

What is the unnamed obstacle in your biggest decision right now?
