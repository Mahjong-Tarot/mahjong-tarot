Feel Good Friday.

At the 2026 Kentucky Derby, a horse named Ocelli was not supposed to be in the race. Another horse got scratched at the gate in the final minutes. The slot opened. Ocelli stepped in, with jockey Ashley Durr in the saddle.

Neither of them knew Friday morning that they would be in the Derby on Saturday. But they were ready. Ashley Durr rides full-time. Ocelli's team had kept him in condition. When the door opened, there was no scramble. They just stepped through.

He came third. At 70-to-1.

The Fire Horse year doesn't care who you were last year. It cares whether you're ready now.

Here's what I want you to take into this weekend: readiness is not a destination you arrive at all at once. You build it in pieces. The preparation you have already done, in whatever direction, is already working. You are probably closer to ready than you think.

The weekend challenge from today's post: write down one thing you are almost ready for, where the remaining gap could be closed in thirty days. Then write what you would actually do if the opportunity arrived tomorrow.

If the answer is clear, you're closer than you thought. If it's unclear, the work is in front of you, and the year still has time.
