## FAQ Schema
**Q: Why is the Wood Sheep month called the month of emotions?**
A: In Chinese astrology, the Sheep (or Goat) is the sign most associated with emotion and love. Combined with the Wood Sheep month's element interplay (wood feeding the year's fire), this makes the month unusually emotionally charged for everyone, not only new parents.

**Q: What is the Feel Good Friday weekend challenge for this week?**
A: Pick one person, a baby, partner, parent, or friend, and give them undivided emotional attention sometime this weekend. Put the phone away, notice the feeling underneath what they say, and respond with one specific, real gesture rather than a vague one.

**Q: How does this challenge apply to parents of a Soulmate-Month baby?**
A: The same principle of full attention applies, but the specific practice is noticing the baby's emotional cues (a particular kind of fuss, a need for closeness) rather than only physical needs like hunger, and responding to that emotional need consistently.
