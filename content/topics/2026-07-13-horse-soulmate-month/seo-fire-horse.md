## FAQ Schema
**Q: What is the Soulmate Month in Chinese astrology?**
A: It's the Wood Sheep month, running roughly July 7 to August 7, 2026. The month sign (Sheep) and the year sign (Horse) are considered soulmates, and both are fire signs, making it the most love-charged month of the Fire Horse year.

**Q: Is the Soulmate Month a safe time for relationships?**
A: Not automatically. The same double-fire energy that deepens real relationships can also fuel temptation and short tempers. It rewards attention and self-control, not passivity.

**Q: What is the Peach Blossom card in a Mahjong reading?**
A: The Peach is the card of romantic temptation and affairs. Bill reports seeing it appear in the problem position of readings more often during this month than any other.

**Q: Which signs are most at risk during the Soulmate Month?**
A: The Horse, Rat, Ox, Monkey, and Rooster carry the most friction into this month, either from amplified restlessness (Horse) or from a harder year overall (Rat, Ox, Monkey, Rooster).
