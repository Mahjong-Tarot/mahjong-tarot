Laying low does not mean doing nothing.

The Snake month is the hardest stretch in the Fire Horse year, but "lay low" is not advice to disappear. It's advice to redirect. And the Mahjong Mirror gives you exactly the framework to do that.

Three moves for the month:

Name what you're tempted to risk. Get it on paper. Not to evaluate it yet. Just to see it clearly. That's the first act of the Mirror: honest inventory.

Go deep on what you already have. A relationship on autopilot. A project mid-stride. A conversation you've been meaning to have. This is the month for depth, not width.

Let honesty carry the weight. The Snake month rewards the people who keep it straight and punishes the people managing a version of events. Get ahead of that. Choose the fuller truth before the month finds the omission for you.

These three moves turn a "wait it out" month into a month of genuine consolidation. You come out the other side with clarity about what you have and what you can move forward on.
