## FAQ Schema
**Q: What does the Peach Blossom card mean in a Mahjong Mirror reading?**
A: It represents romantic temptation and affairs. In the problem position, it signals that passion exists but isn't being directed at the right person, not necessarily that infidelity has already occurred.

**Q: What does the Sword card mean?**
A: The Sword signals that something in a relationship isn't working and the honest move is to end it rather than keep patching it.

**Q: What is the Second Angle of the Mahjong Mirror?**
A: Know Thyself. It asks who you actually are in a relationship right now, honestly, rather than who you want to be or who you were in the past.

**Q: What is the Third Angle of the Mahjong Mirror?**
A: The Opposition. It asks what is really standing in the way of a relationship, beneath the surface argument, so you can address the true obstacle instead of repeating the same fight.
