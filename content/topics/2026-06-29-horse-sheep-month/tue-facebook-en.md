A little more on the Soulmate Month, because "soulmate" doesn't always mean easy.

Some of the biggest moments of your year are going to land between July 7 and August 7. Some of them will be wonderful. A few will be hard lessons. That's what a Soulmate Month does: it concentrates the significant stuff, not just the good stuff.

Even the favored signs (Horse, Pig, Rabbit) should be confident but careful. Don't go hog wild. The passion of this month is real, and so is the temptation to let it run the show. The people who get the most out of the Soulmate Month are the ones who know what they want going in, not just how they feel in the moment.

This month rewards passion paired with self-control. Not passion without it.
