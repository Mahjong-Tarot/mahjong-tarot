Effort isn't the same as progress.

The flailing surfer proves it. He is working harder than anyone else in the water. He is trying more. He is absolutely committed. And he falls in every time.

Wu Wei is not about doing less. It's about doing what's aligned.

In the Fire Horse year, more force is sometimes the answer. But more force applied in the wrong direction is just faster flailing.

The question is not: am I working hard enough?

The question is: am I aligned with what's actually happening right now?

That's the whole text of the Tao Te Ching in one question.
