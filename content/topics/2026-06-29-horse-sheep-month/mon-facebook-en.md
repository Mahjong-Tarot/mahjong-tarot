The hardest stretch of the Fire Horse year is almost behind us.

On July 7 the Wood Sheep month begins. And this one has a name: the Soulmate Month. That's what happens when the month sign and the year sign are soulmates. Major life events cluster, and love takes center stage.

Two fire signs together means a lot of passion. The Sheep is the sign of emotions. The Horse is fire through and through. July is the month for lovers: to deepen what you have, to take a relationship to the next level, or to find someone new.

The one rule: where passion runs high, tempers can too. Self-control is what separates the people who make the most of this month from the ones who let the heat make their decisions.

Not every sign has the same experience. About half are favored (Horse, Pig, Rabbit, Dragon, Dog, Snake). About half will need to tend their garden (Rat, Ox, Tiger, Monkey, Rooster).
