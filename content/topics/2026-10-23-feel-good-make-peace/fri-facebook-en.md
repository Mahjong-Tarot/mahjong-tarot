Feel Good Friday: you already know who it is.

The moment you read the words "make peace," a face came to mind. A sister. An old friend. Someone you stopped calling after this very loud year.

The Sheep is a peaceful sign. Next year is a good one for conflict resolution, and the odds are in your favor. So why wait for February?

This weekend, pick one person and go first. You don't need a speech. One small, gentle sentence is enough: "I was thinking about you."

Who's your one person? You don't have to give me a name. Just tell me you're going to do it.
