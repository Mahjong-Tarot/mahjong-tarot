## FAQ Schema

**Q: What does the Mahjong Mirror say to do in a difficult month?**
A: The Mirror redirects energy rather than suppressing it. In the Snake month, three moves apply: name what you're tempted to risk (so you're choosing patience consciously), go deep on existing relationships and projects (depth over width), and use honesty as an active discipline to avoid the month's biggest trap.

**Q: What is the Mahjong Mirror framework?**
A: The Mahjong Mirror is a four-angle decision-making framework created by Bill Hajdu. The four angles are: the central theme (what is actually at stake), Know Thyself (who you are in this situation), the opposition (what is working against you), and the future (what the decision is building toward). It uses Mahjong tiles to surface honest answers to each question.

**Q: How do you navigate a "lay low" month in Chinese astrology?**
A: Laying low means not starting new ventures or taking big risks. Not doing nothing. The productive moves are: inventory what you're tempted to chase and consciously wait on it; deepen attention to existing relationships and work; default to honesty in all communications. These redirect the month's energy constructively.

**Q: Why does the Snake month reward honesty?**
A: The Snake animal is associated with perception and revelation. In a high-fire year (like the Fire Horse), the consequences of dishonesty are amplified. Small deceptions that might pass unnoticed in a calmer year get exposed and cause disproportionate damage. Choosing honesty preemptively protects relationships and reputation.
