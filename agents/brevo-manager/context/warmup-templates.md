# Warmup Email Templates — Bill's voice

Plain-text openers and subject lines for the FirePig sender domain warmup. Used by the `firepig-warmup-daily` scheduled task. Edit freely — Bill's actual voice trumps anything written here.

## Tone rules

- Short. One thing per email. Never more than 4 sentences.
- Lowercase subject lines, no exclamation marks.
- Ask a question that invites a real reply.
- Mention concrete details from this person's life — not generic.
- No "Hope you're doing well." It's the spam-flag of personal email.
- Sign just "Bill". No title, no link, no quote.

## Subject lines — rotate, don't repeat

- `quick question`
- `been a while`
- `are you still in [place]?`
- `[their name] — random`
- `no agenda — just saying hi`
- `coffee?`
- `[city] in [month]?`
- `that thing you said about [topic]`

## Phase 1 openers (May 12–18, from `firepig@onlinechineseastrology.com`)

Pick one, fill in real details:

1. **`Hey [name], been a while. How's [the thing they're working on]?`**
2. **`[Name] — saw [specific thing] and thought of you. Are you still doing [thing]?`**
3. **`Hey [name], real quick — are you still in [city]?`**
4. **`[Name] — random question. Did you ever finish [book / project / move]?`**
5. **`Hey [name]. Years late on this but — how did [event] turn out?`**
6. **`Bill here. Been meaning to write. How's [family member / pet / specific situation]?`**
7. **`Hey [name] — quick one. Are you free for coffee or a call in the next few weeks?`**
8. **`[Name] — I'm working through old emails today and you came up. What are you reading lately?`**
9. **`Hey [name], are you still painting / brewing / coaching / writing?`**

## Closing line — pick one

- `Bill`
- `Bill\n\n— Bill`
- `Bill\n\n(reply if you can — I'm trying to dust off old habits)`

## Anti-patterns — avoid

- "Hope this finds you well" — generic
- "Just checking in" — empty
- Anything with a P.S. about a project, book, link, or product (during Phases 1-2b only)
- HTML signatures with logos
- Auto-bcc'd anyone
- Same opener to two people in a row
- Mailto links or calendar links in the body

## Reply hygiene

When they reply (and ~50% will):

- Reply same day, even if short ("Yes! Tell me more." / "Will write you a longer one this weekend.")
- Quoting their message is fine. Top-post the reply.
- DO NOT add a Brevo unsubscribe footer. These are personal emails from Mail.app / Gmail, not bulk.

The reply is the most valuable signal — the deliverability gods are watching the back-and-forth, not the send count.
