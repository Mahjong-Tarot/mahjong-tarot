Feel Good Friday: I've been hard on the Sheep all week, so let me say this. Sheep people can be really darling, sweet people.

They can also drive you up the wall.

The trick isn't changing them. Nobody has managed that yet. The trick is giving them what they need. Five ways: find what lights their fire, give a push instead of a shove, don't preach, lend the shoulder, and work out what they actually need.

This weekend, pick one person in your life and do just one of these once before Monday.

Who's your Sheep? Tell me which one you're going to try.
