# The Dark Side of the Sheep (2027, the Fire Sheep)

**Source:** Bill × Dave recording, 2026-10-05 (Mahjong Mirror interview, 24:48 to 33:22). Lark Minutes: https://edge8company.sg.larksuite.com/minutes/obsgftv266m7eb2tivqyh4w3
**Begins:** February 6, 2027
**Post angle:** Every sign has a good side and a dark side. Everybody wants to hear about the good side, and the big-name salesmen of astrology make everything sound brimming with possibilities. Bill likes to look at the dark side, both of the Sheep year and of the Sheep person.

## The Sheep year's dark side
- **Number one: the Sheep is emotional, whiny, clingy, needy.** People born in the year of the Rat are going to have a really hard time dealing with people exhibiting this behavior.
- If you are the more logical, rational kind of person, you can feel quite uncomfortable next year. You may be the rational type and still find yourself getting too emotional, too involved, losing self-control, not in control of situations the way you normally are. That can throw a monkey wrench in your plans: your own behavior, but especially the behavior of people around you.
- **The Sheep is not good at making money necessarily, but it is sure good at spending it.** We will all tend to overspend and stretch ourselves too thin. Balancing the budget and keeping an eye on cash flow are really important, because the flow is to not pay attention and end up losing money.
- **It is really about self-control in general.** The Sheep is a fire sign. Even though it is a relatively passive Yin sign, it can do reckless things. The area where recklessness could be most disastrous is money.
- **The Sheep is not into planning.** It isn't really into the logic and thinking that goes into planning. Free-spirited, winging it. People preaching plans are going to have a hard sell next year. Spontaneity bordering on recklessness.

## The Sheep person's dark side
- You might think a Sheep person doesn't have a lot of motivation, and maybe they don't. Even though the Sheep is a fire sign, they need something to light their fire and they need a push. Bill doesn't want to say lazy. They are not a go-getter by nature.
- Not everyone born in a Sheep year is like that. The more your other signs strengthen your Sheep sign, the more likely you are to have this problem.
- Carefree and sensitive. Sensitive to the point of being whiny and a big crybaby.
- When dealing with a Sheep person, help them with their confidence. Don't preach to them. Make them feel in a way that they gain confidence by themselves.
- They need emotional support, a shoulder to lean on. Even when they know what they are doing and are competent, they still want to lean on you. That can be aggravating.
- "The Sheep can be really darling, sweet people, but they can drive you up the wall too." Show some empathy and figure out what they need emotionally. They can be draining.
