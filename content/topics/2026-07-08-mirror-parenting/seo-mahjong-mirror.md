## FAQ Schema
**Q: What are the four pillars in Chinese astrology?**
A: The four pillars (Ba Zi) are the year, month, day, and hour of birth. Each pillar contributes a different layer to a person's personality and life energy. Most people only consider the birth year (their animal sign), but the month and day pillars add important nuance. A baby born in 2026 is a firehorse by year, but may also carry a Wood, Water, Earth, or Metal energy from their birth month and day.

**Q: Why is parenting a firehorse different from parenting other signs?**
A: The firehorse (born in a Fire Horse year) carries double fire energy -- the most passionate and high-intensity combination in the 60-year Chinese zodiac cycle. The child is likely to have high energy, short attention span, and strong emotional responses. Of all 60 signs, firehorse children are most likely to bring both the greatest joy and the greatest challenge to their parents.

**Q: How does the Mahjong Mirror framework apply to parenting?**
A: The Mahjong Mirror is a decision-making framework built on looking closely at the real situation and customizing your response. Applied to parenting, this means reading all four pillars (not just the birth year), watching how your specific child actually responds rather than applying a universal technique, and staying attentive to changes in the child's behavior as the monthly energy shifts.

**Q: What is a double horse or triple horse baby?**
A: In Chinese astrology, when a child's month and/or day of birth also falls on the Horse sign within a Horse year, those layers stack. A double horse baby is born in a Horse month of a Horse year. A triple horse is born on a Horse day in a Horse month of a Horse year. The stacking amplifies both the positive traits and the challenges of the Horse sign.

**Q: How do the months of 2026 affect a firehorse baby?**
A: Each month has its own animal and element energy that layers over the year's Fire Horse energy. The Wood Sheep month (July 7 to August 7) is the year's soulmate month -- slightly calmer fire but more emotionally demanding. The Dog month (approximately October 8 to November 6) is a complementary, more relaxed energy that can help a firehorse child slow down.
