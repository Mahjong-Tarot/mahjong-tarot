## FAQ Schema
**Q: Is a baby born in the Soulmate Month a double horse?**
A: No. The double and triple horse window was the Wood Horse month, which ran roughly June 6 to July 7, 2026. A baby born after July 7, during the Wood Sheep month (the Soulmate Month), is a Horse-year, Sheep-month baby -- a different, and separately auspicious, combination.

**Q: Why is the Wood Sheep month called the Soulmate Month?**
A: The Sheep is the Horse's soulmate sign in Chinese astrology, meaning the year sign (Horse) and the month sign (Sheep) are highly compatible. When a year and month sign are soulmates, that window tends to carry major life events for everyone, and for a baby born during it, the soulmate month becomes a permanent part of their chart.

**Q: Is a Soulmate-Month baby easier to parent than a firehorse baby?**
A: Not necessarily easier, just different. The Sheep is a gentler, yin fire compared to the Horse's yang fire, which calms some of the raw energy and crying. But the Sheep is also the sign of emotions, so the baby needs significantly more attention, stroking, and emotional support than a typical firehorse baby.

**Q: What does "wood feeds fire" mean for a baby's chart?**
A: In the five-element system, wood generates fire. The Wood Sheep month's element (wood) feeds the Fire Horse year's element (fire), which is considered a highly favorable combination -- one reason the source material describes something important and meaningful happening early for babies born in this window.

**Entity: Month pillar vs. year pillar in Ba Zi**

The month pillar adds a permanent layer -- for babies born July 7 to August 7, 2026, that layer is Wood Sheep, the soulmate sign, associated with emotion and love.

**Direct answer: Is the Soulmate-Month baby easier to raise than the firehorse baby?**
No. The Soulmate-Month baby carries a gentler, yin-fire energy that reduces some of the raw crying and hyperactivity typical of a firehorse baby, but the Sheep is the sign of emotions, so this baby needs more attention, more physical comfort, and more emotional responsiveness than average. The calm is real. So is the increased need for presence.
