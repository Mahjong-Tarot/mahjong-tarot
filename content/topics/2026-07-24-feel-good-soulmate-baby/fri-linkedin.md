# Friday LinkedIn: July 24, 2026

A small challenge to close the week: give one person your undivided attention before Sunday night.

This week's posts covered the Wood Sheep month in Chinese astrology, the "Soulmate Month," and why the baby born in it needs more emotional presence than any other child born this year. But the underlying lesson isn't limited to new parents. The Sheep is the sign of emotion, and this month raises the emotional stakes for every relationship, professional ones included.

The challenge is simple and deliberately small: pick one person, put the phone away, and notice what's underneath what they're actually saying rather than just responding to the words. Then follow up with one specific, real action, not a vague gesture. It's a small practice with an outsized return, in a month built on emotional intensity or otherwise.
