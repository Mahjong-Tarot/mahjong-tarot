# Q3 2026 — The Three-Month Arc: Metal Dragon → Metal Snake → Water Horse

**Source:** Bill × Dave recording, 2026-05-23
**Covers:** mid-Apr to mid-Jul 2026, inside the Year of the Fire Horse
**Post angle:** A high-level "what to expect this quarter" piece linking the three months.

## The arc
- **Metal Dragon** — Apr 17 to May 16 (just ended)
- **Metal Snake** — May 17 to Jun 14 (current)
- **Water Horse** — Jun 15 onward (next)

Two metal months and one water month, all unfolding inside the Fire Horse year.

## The core mechanic
The year's **fire energy destroys metal's luck.** That puts luck against you through both metal months — the Dragon and the Snake.

## Month by month
- **Metal Dragon — workable.** Dragon and Horse are a reasonably compatible pairing. Despite the fire-vs-metal drag, the Dragon month can still deliver real, even big, successes. It is the most opportunity-bearing of the three.
- **Metal Snake — the turn.** The mood flips. Snake and Horse are not compatible; the Snake dislikes the Horse's pace and its habit of leaping ahead without thinking. Both are fire signs, and in a fire year that means too much fire — stress, with particular strain on relationships.
- **Water Horse — double down.** A Water Horse month inside a Fire Horse year doubles the horse energy and adds the volatile water-fire clash.

## Takeaway
Three challenging months back to back. The Dragon window is where to place anything ambitious; ease off through the Snake and Horse months.
