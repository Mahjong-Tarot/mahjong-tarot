Most of us have a career, not a business. So here's the career rule for 2027.

People are going to be sensitive next year. That makes relationships at work matter more than usual. A sharp word lands harder and gets remembered longer.

Passion is a double-edged sword at work. Channel the fire into the work and it can make 2027 your best year. Let it pull you into conflict and it can make it your worst.

Two rules I'd tape to your monitor:
1. When you find yourself in a conflict, walk away. Say it later, when the heat is gone.
2. Never get into a personality conflict with your boss. At all costs.

Which one is harder for you?
