# Friday Facebook (EN) - May 15

Feel Good Friday.

The Fire Horse year doesn't end quietly. It ends with consequences. The quality of those consequences depends on what you did with the year so far.

We're past the midpoint. The Sheep year follows the Horse. Always. The Sheep year is quiet, reflective, sustainable. It builds on what you built in the Fire Horse year.

So here's your challenge for this weekend: one question, and give yourself an honest answer.

**What am I actively building in my love life right now?**

Not hoping for. Not planning to get around to. Building.

If you can answer that clearly, you're in good shape. If the answer is "I'm not sure," that's your information. The Fire Horse rewards the ones who decide and move. The Sheep year rewards the ones who built something real.

You still have time. Use it.

What are you building? Drop it in the comments.
