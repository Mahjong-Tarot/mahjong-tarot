## FAQ Schema
**Q: When does the Fire Horse year end and the Sheep year begin?**
A: The 2026 Fire Horse year runs from February 17, 2026 to February 5, 2027. The Sheep (Goat) year then begins. In Chinese astrology, the Sheep year is characterized by quietness, reflection, and artistic, sustainable energy. This is a significant shift from the Fire Horse's intensity.

**Q: Does the Sheep year affect love differently than the Fire Horse year?**
A: Yes. The Sheep year brings softer, more reflective energy to relationships. While the Fire Horse amplifies passion and volatility, the Sheep year supports sustainable connection and deepening bonds. What is built or broken during the Fire Horse year sets the conditions for the Sheep year's relational experience.

**Q: What should I do if my love life has been difficult during the Fire Horse year?**

**Q: What does "tend the garden" mean in Chinese astrology?**
A: "Tend the garden" is Bill Hajdu's phrase for actively nurturing a relationship. Not assuming that a good relationship will maintain itself without attention. In the Fire Horse year, this active tending is especially important because the year's energy amplifies neglect as readily as it amplifies passion.
