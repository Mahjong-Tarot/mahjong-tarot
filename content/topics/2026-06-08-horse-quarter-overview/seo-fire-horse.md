**Q: What is the Water Horse month in Chinese astrology?**
A: The Water Horse month runs June 15 onward in 2026. Inside the Fire Horse year, a Water Horse month doubles the Horse energy (year Horse plus month Horse) and adds the volatile water-and-fire dynamic. **Q: What are the three months in Q3 2026 in Chinese astrology?**
A: Metal Dragon (April 17–May 16), Metal Snake (May 17–June 14), and Water Horse (June 15 onward). All three months run inside the Fire Horse year, with the year's fire energy working against metal's luck in the first two months.

**Q: Why was the Metal Dragon month good despite the fire-versus-metal problem?**
A: The Dragon's inherent ambitious energy is large enough to partly offset the elemental drag. A month delivering even partial Dragon potential still produces significant opportunity. The Dragon month was the quarter's best window for bold moves.

**Q: What happens in a Water Horse month inside a Fire Horse year?**
A: The Horse energy doubles (year Horse plus month Horse) and the water-fire dynamic creates volatility. Things moving in a good direction accelerate; things that have been off-track become clearly off-track. It rewards people who are well-prepared and exposes unresolved problems from the Snake month.

**Q: How should I prepare for the Water Horse month?**
A: Use the final week of the Snake month (before June 14) to resolve anything unfinished: have the honest conversation you've been postponing, secure what you built in the Dragon month, identify what you want to protect and carry forward. Arrive at June 15 with clarity about your position.
