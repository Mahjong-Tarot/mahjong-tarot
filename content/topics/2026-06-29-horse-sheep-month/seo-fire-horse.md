## FAQ Schema

**Q: What is the Soulmate Month in Chinese astrology?**
A: The Soulmate Month occurs when the Chinese month sign and the year sign are soulmates. In 2026, the Wood Sheep month (July 7 to August 7) is the Soulmate Month within the Fire Horse year. Major life events, especially in love and relationships, tend to cluster in this window.

**Q: What does the Wood Sheep month mean for relationships in 2026?**
A: The Wood Sheep month is the most love-charged stretch of the Fire Horse year. Wood feeds fire (the year element), creating an auspicious combination. The Sheep is the sign of emotions and love, and the Sheep and Horse are both fire signs, meaning passion runs high. It's the best window of the year to deepen a relationship, take one to the next level, or start something new. High passion also means high tempers, so self-control matters.

**Q: Which signs do best in the Wood Sheep month 2026?**
A: The most favorable signs are Horse, Pig, and Rabbit (Cat in Vietnamese), followed by Dragon, Dog, and Snake. About half the signs are favored. The other six (Rat, Ox, Tiger, Monkey, Rooster) face more challenges and should focus on tending existing relationships rather than seeking new ones.

**Q: When does the Wood Sheep month 2026 begin?**
A: The Wood Sheep month begins on July 7, 2026, at the Xiaoshu solar term, and runs to approximately August 7, 2026.

**Q: What is the main warning for the Soulmate Month?**
A: High passion means high tempers. Two fire signs (Sheep and Horse) together create intense energy in all directions. The single most important rule for every sign during this month is to lead with self-control, especially in arguments with people you care about.
