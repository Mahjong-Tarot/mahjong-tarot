Feel Good Friday: name one thing to carry into the Sheep year.

Not a list. One thing. A habit you want running in 2027. A relationship you're choosing to tend right now. A practice that keeps you steady when the emotional energy of the year runs high. A financial discipline that holds.

The Fire Horse year is still running. Yang energy, decisive and strong, is still available. The Sheep year rewards what you build in the loud one. Not what you assemble after the door opens.

So this weekend: name your one thing and take one small step toward it. Call the person. Write the first line. Make the decision you've been circling. Ten minutes toward the thing you want to carry in.

The fire still has something to give. Use it before it goes.

What's yours? Drop it in the comments.
