More from the parenting series: the firehorse child's number one need is to learn to relax.

That sounds simple. It is actually a real skill -- one you have to teach deliberately, because the firehorse's natural setting is full speed. Have your soothing techniques ready: calming music, holding, rocking. Start building your toolkit now if you have not already. And if you are going to try the cry-it-out approach with a firehorse baby, be prepared, because the volume and the persistence of that cry may be more than you expected.

The other side of this is the home itself. In Chinese astrology, feng shui is not a decorating decision. It is the space side of the same wisdom. The year asks questions about time; feng shui answers questions about space. For a firehorse baby: calming colors, a clean and quiet room layout, everything in the environment tuned to help them wind down. Build the pocket of calm before the baby needs it.
