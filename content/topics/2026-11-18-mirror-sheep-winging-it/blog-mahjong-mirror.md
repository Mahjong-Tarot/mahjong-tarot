# The Sheep Doesn't Plan. That's Why You Have To.

My son Dave goes around teaching people to make a clear plan before they start anything. He's good at it. He's also about to have a rough year, because I told him the truth: all that preaching about plans is going to be a hard sell in 2027.

Next year belongs to the Sheep, and the Sheep doesn't plan.

I'm the Firepig. In over 35 years of doing readings, I've seen people get hurt by bad luck and I've seen people get hurt by a good mood. The second one is harder to spot while it's happening. [On Monday](/blog/posts/dark-side-of-the-sheep-year-2027) I laid out the dark side of the Fire Sheep. Today I want to take the most expensive piece of it and show you what to do about it.

## What "Winging It" Actually Means

The Sheep isn't really into the logic and the thinking that goes into planning. It's a free spirit. It goes along, sees what happens, and figures it'll work out.

That's charming at a picnic. It's a lot less charming in your bank account.

When a whole year takes on that quality, you feel it even if you're a careful person. The budget meeting gets pushed. The thing you were going to think over gets decided on a whim. Somebody suggests something, it feels right, and off you go. It's spontaneity, and it's bordering on recklessness, and from the inside it feels like being relaxed.

That last part is the trouble. Recklessness in a Horse year feels like recklessness. In a Sheep year it feels like a nice afternoon.

## How a Passive Sign Gets Reckless

People get confused here, so let me explain it.

The Sheep is a Yin sign and a relatively passive one. You'd expect passive to mean safe. It doesn't, because the Sheep is also a fire sign, and 2027 is a fire year. Put fire underneath a sign that doesn't think ahead and you get something unusual: an impulse with no plan attached to it.

The Horse charges at trouble with its eyes open. The Sheep just isn't paying attention until the trouble has moved in.

> You don't have to do anything dramatic to get hurt in a Sheep year. You only have to stop looking.

## Why Your Money Takes the Hit

Recklessness could show up anywhere next year. The place it could be most disastrous is money.

The Sheep is not necessarily good at making money. It is sure good at spending it. So we're all going to have a tendency to overspend and stretch ourselves too thin. I've already given you [the one word for money next year](/blog/posts/fire-sheep-2027-money-career-caution), and I'm not going to repeat that sermon. Today's point is narrower. Caution is an attitude. Attitudes don't survive a good mood. You need something on paper.

Two boring things are going to matter more next year than they have in a long time.

**Balance the budget.** Know what's coming in and what's going out, and write it down while you're calm.

**Keep an eye on cash flow.** Not just what you own. What you can actually pay this month and next.

Neither of these is exciting, and that's exactly the problem. The flow of a Sheep year is to not pay attention to them and end up losing money. Nobody decides to stop watching their cash flow. It just slips, one pleasant week at a time.

## A Plan for a Year That Hates Plans

Here's what I've learned about people and plans. You can hand someone a perfect spreadsheet in a Sheep year, and it will sit there. The mood of the year is against it.

So you need a plan that doesn't feel like one. That's what the Mahjong Mirror is. It looks at any decision from four angles, and each one is just a question. A question will get through when a spreadsheet won't.

Take the next unplanned thing you're tempted to spend on, sign up for, or say yes to. Then ask.

**The Central Theme: what is actually at stake here?** Not the price tag. The real thing. Is this purchase about the object, or about a rough week? Is this investment about the return, or about not wanting to miss out?

**Know Thyself: who are you in this decision?** Are you calm or are you carried away? The Sheep year fills the room with feeling, and it's very easy to mistake your mood for your judgment.

**The Opposition: what is working against you?** Next year the answer often has nothing to do with the world outside. What's working against you is the flow of the year itself, telling you it'll all work out.

**The Future: what does this build toward?** Follow it forward. Where does this leave your budget, and your peace of mind, a few months from now?

Four questions at the kitchen table. That's the plan. In a reading I use Mahjong cards to surface the honest answers, because people will tell a card things they won't admit to themselves. But you can start with just the questions.

Notice what the Mirror doesn't ask you to do. It doesn't ask you to stop being spontaneous. It asks you to stop for a minute first.

## Keep the Fun. Protect the Money.

I'm not telling you to spend 2027 as a human spreadsheet. The Sheep has a lot to offer, and a year with some ease in it is a welcome change after what the Fire Horse has put us through.

Wing it on what you'll have for dinner. Wing it on the scenic route.

Don't wing it on the money. The Sheep year arrives on February 6, and you have time right now to get your budget on paper, look hard at your cash flow, and get in the habit of asking four questions before you say yes. Do it while your head is still cool.
