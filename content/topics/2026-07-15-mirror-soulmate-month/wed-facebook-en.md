Two cards keep showing up in my readings this month: the Peach and the Sword. Neither one is comfortable. Both are clarifying.

The Peach isn't a verdict that someone's cheating. It usually means the fire is looking for somewhere to go and hasn't decided where yet. The Sword shows up when something isn't working and the honest move is to end it, not keep patching it.

The Mirror's Second Angle, Know Thyself, asks who you actually are in your relationship right now. The Third Angle, The Opposition, asks what's really standing between you and your partner, not the surface argument. I walked through both on the blog today: https://www.mahjongtarot.com/blog/posts/what-the-peach-blossom-and-the-sword-are-telling-you
