In 35 years of doing readings, I've sat across from thousands of people who were exhausted by a year that was going well.

Not failing. Not falling apart. Just working twice as hard as necessary because they were moving against the energy instead of with it. Pushing in months that wanted patience. Holding back in months that wanted action. Fighting the current when the current was the whole point.

The Fire Horse year is the most energetic year in 60 years. That energy isn't neutral. It has a direction every single month. And the most important choice you can make in a year like this is to stop fighting the direction and start using it.

## The Month Doesn't Care About Your Schedule

This is the thing that most people resist, and I understand why.

You have a calendar. You have commitments. You have a business plan, a personal goal, a project deadline, a conversation you keep scheduling and rescheduling. The month you're in doesn't pause your plans while you figure out whether its energy is with you or against you.

I know that. The question is not whether you can match every action to its perfect astrological window. You can't. Nobody can.

The question is whether you can shift the center of gravity of your effort toward the months that want the energy you're spending.

The Dragon month wanted boldness. Did your boldest moves happen in April? Or did you spend April being cautious and then wonder why May felt like you missed something?

The Snake month wants depth and patience. Are you spending this month trying to launch things and force momentum, and wondering why everything is heavier than it should be?

The Water Horse month, opening June 15, wants prepared action. Specific, ready, clear. Are you preparing for it, or are you going to arrive at June 15 still carrying the Snake month's unresolved friction?

That's the question. Not "can I restructure my whole life around Chinese astrology?" but "am I swimming with the current or against it?"

## Why Planning With the Current Is a Form of Respect

Here is what I believe about this, after 35 years.

People who work with the year's energy aren't passive. They're not waiting for permission from the stars. They're not handing their agency over to a cycle.

They're doing something harder: honest self-assessment of where they're spending effort and whether that's where the energy is actually flowing.

Working with the current takes more discipline than swimming against it. Because swimming against it feels like trying hard. It feels like determination. It feels like you're not giving up.

Working with the current requires you to know what the current is doing right now (this month, this week) and to trust that pausing an ambitious move during a consolidation month is not a failure of will. It's the kind of patience that delivers results instead of exhaustion.

The Fire Horse year will reward you for knowing the difference.

## Your Weekend Challenge

Map the next six weeks on paper. It takes fifteen minutes. It doesn't require a detailed plan for every day. Just the broad energy of each month.

Snake month: now through June 14. Consolidation. What are you protecting? What honest conversations haven't happened yet?

Water Horse month: June 15 onward. Movement. What are you ready to push? What bold move have you been preparing?

Now pick two things.

One bold move you will make once the Water Horse opens. Not a vague intention. A specific, named action. A decision you'll commit to. A conversation you'll have. A step you'll take.

One thing you will hold until the month's energy is with you. The risky move you've been tempted to push through the Snake month. The launch that belongs in a different window.

That's the whole challenge. A bold move and a hold. Planned with the current, not against it.

The people who come out of a hard quarter strong are not the ones who pushed hardest every day. They're the ones who knew when to push and when to hold. And had the discipline to do both.

You already have the information. The Water Horse is one week away.
