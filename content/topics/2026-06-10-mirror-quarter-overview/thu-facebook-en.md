The mistake in a hard quarter is treating every month the same.

Here's a practical version of the Mirror's sequencing question that you can use right now, before June 15.

Write down three things:

One: what bold move do you want to make once the Water Horse month opens? Not the idea. The specific move, the specific decision, the specific conversation. Name it.

Two: what are you carrying from the Snake month that needs resolution before June 15? Not the full list. The one thing most pressing. The unresolved conversation. The decision that didn't actually get made. Name it.

Three: what is the one thing you built during the Dragon month that you want to protect and carry forward? Name it.

That's the Mirror's pre-work for the Water Horse. Three things on paper. You know where you are. You know what you're carrying. You know what you want to move when the energy returns.
