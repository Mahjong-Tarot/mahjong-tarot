I'm the Firepig. Nobody gets to pick the month their baby arrives. I've never once had a client ask me to time a birth around a favorable lunar month, and if they did, I'd tell them the same thing I'm about to tell you: that's not how this works, and it's not what the Mahjong Mirror is for.

What the Mirror is for is reading, clearly, what's already written and what's coming next. Monday I told you the Soulmate-Month baby carries the most auspicious combination of the year and the most emotional need to go with it. That's the diagnosis. Today I want to show you the actual map, because the month pillar isn't the end of the story. It's one layer in a sequence that keeps moving for the rest of the year.

<h2>Two Pillars, Not One</h2>

Quick recap, because this matters and I don't want to assume everyone read Monday's post closely. A birth chart has four pillars: year, month, day, and more. The year pillar is the engine. For every baby born in 2026, that engine is Fire Horse: energy, passion, freedom, movement. The month pillar is a second, permanent layer sitting right next to it. For a baby born July 7 through August 7, that layer is Wood Sheep, the soulmate sign, carrying emotion and love.

Year says how much engine. Month says how much emotion. Those two pillars don't compete. They combine, permanently, into the child you're actually raising.

<h2>What Amplifies: The Two Wood Months</h2>

Here's where I want to widen the lens beyond just the Soulmate Month, because Bill's brief on this doesn't treat months as isolated events. It maps them as a sequence, and it specifically calls out which ones amplify the firehorse traits and which one balances them.

The first amplifier was the Wood Horse month, running roughly June 6 to July 7. A baby born there is a double horse, sometimes a triple horse on specific days. Maximum stacking of the same sign, maximum intensity.

The second amplifier is the month we're in right now, the Wood Sheep month, running July 7 through August 7. Different mechanic than the double horse, but still an amplifier in its own way: two wood months back to back, both feeding the year's fire, both raising the emotional and energetic stakes for any firehorse-year baby, not only the ones born inside this specific window. If your child was born in May, the ambient Wood Sheep energy moving through the world right now is still touching them. It's not just about the babies born in this exact 30-day stretch. It's the weather everyone's living inside.

<h2>What Balances: The Metal Dog Window</h2>

Now the part I actually want you circling on your calendar. Later this year, roughly October 8 through November 6, the Dog month arrives. Metal Dog, specifically.

The Dog is a naturally relaxed sign, and it complements the Horse rather than clashing with it. It encourages the Horse to slow down instead of fighting the Horse's instincts head-on. Add the element layer: the Dog month itself carries metal, and in the five-element cycle, fire controls metal. The year's fire, the Horse, holds the upper hand over the month's metal energy rather than being overwhelmed by it. Put a naturally calming animal sign together with an element relationship that keeps the year's fire in charge, and you get exactly what the brief calls it: the calming window of the year.

That's not a small thing if you've been in the thick of a demanding firehorse or soulmate-month baby since spring. Roughly eight weeks of relief are already on the calendar. You don't have to guess when the reprieve is coming. You can read it.

<h2>What Happens Between the Wood Months and the Dog</h2>

I want to be honest about something rather than paper over it. Bill's brief doesn't hand you a scorecard for every single month of 2026. It flags the ones that matter most: two that amplify, one that calms. The stretch between the Wood Sheep month closing on August 7 and the Metal Dog month opening October 8 isn't covered in the same detail, and I'm not going to invent a rating for it just to fill a chart.

That's actually the lesson, not a gap in it. The Mirror doesn't ask you to have an opinion about every month whether the material supports one or not. It asks you to know clearly what you do know, plan around it, and stay observant during the stretches where the picture is less defined. A good reading tells you where the strong signals are. It doesn't manufacture certainty where there isn't any.

<blockquote>
Don't label months good or bad. Read them, and parent to what's actually in front of you.
</blockquote>

<h2>The Mirror's Discipline: Read It, Don't Label It</h2>

I want to be careful here, because it would be easy to turn this into a simple scorecard: wood months bad, dog month good. That's not the lesson, and it's not how the Mirror works.

The wood months aren't bad. They're intense, and intensity cuts toward both the joy and the difficulty at once, same as it did on Monday. The Metal Dog month isn't a reward either. It's simply a different kind of month, one where the ambient pressure eases and a naturally calmer energy takes over for a while. The discipline isn't to sort months into good and bad columns. It's to know, specifically, what each one is asking of you, so you're not caught flat-footed by a shift you could have seen coming.

This is the same principle from two weeks ago, when I said you don't parent a firehorse, you parent your child. The chart gives you the map. You still have to read the terrain in front of you as it actually is, month by month, not just year by year.

<h2>What This Actually Means for You Right Now</h2>

If your baby was born in the Wood Sheep window, you're inside the second amplifier as I write this. Expect the emotional demand Monday's post described to stay high through early August. Don't wait for it to ease on its own; it eases when the calendar turns, not before.

If your baby is older, born earlier in the Fire Horse year, know that the ambient wood energy right now is still amplifying their firehorse traits somewhat, even without a fresh month pillar layering on. And for every firehorse-year parent, mark October 8 on your calendar. That's not a random date. That's the start of roughly a month where the year's grip loosens and the Dog's calmer nature gets room to work.

Plan around it. If you've been putting off something demanding, something that requires a settled household, the Metal Dog window is a better bet than the months on either side of it.

<h2>You Don't Have to Guess</h2>

That's the whole point of a chart, and the whole point of the Mirror. You can't pick the month your baby arrives in. You can't move the Metal Dog window earlier because you're tired in September. But you don't have to parent blind, either. The map exists. Read it, and you'll know when to brace and when you're allowed to breathe.
