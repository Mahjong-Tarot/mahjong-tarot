Feel Good Friday.

The Soulmate Month begins July 7. That's four days from now.

And I'm going to give you two moves to make before it does.

First: name one relationship you want to deepen this month. Not fix. Not repair. Deepen. One specific thing you want to do: a conversation you've been avoiding, a shared experience you've been meaning to create, something you've wanted to say and haven't said yet.

Second: name one fight you're going to refuse to pick. Two fire signs together means high passion and high tempers. The arguments that feel most urgent in a month like this are often the ones least worth having. Decide now, before the heat decides for you.

Write both down. That's it. The month brings the passion. You bring the self-control.
