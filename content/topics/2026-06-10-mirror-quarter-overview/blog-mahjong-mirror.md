# When to Push and When to Hold: the Mirror Across a Hard Quarter

Here is the mistake most people make in a volatile year.

They treat every month the same. They pick a level of energy (cautious, or ambitious, or somewhere in the middle) and they hold it across the whole calendar. If the year is going well, they push constantly and get burned when the energy shifts. If the year is difficult, they hold back constantly and miss the windows that were actually open.

The Mahjong Mirror was built for exactly this problem.

Not just for single decisions. For sequences. For reading which window wants boldness and which demands patience, and calibrating your effort accordingly. That sequencing intelligence is one of the things that separates the people who navigate a volatile year well from the people who simply endure it.

Here's how to apply the Mirror to the quarter you're in.

## What the Mirror's First Angle Tells You About a Month

The Mirror's first angle asks: what is the central theme? Not the surface question, not the wishful version. What's actually at stake.

When you apply that question to a month rather than a decision, it becomes: what is this month actually for?

The Metal Dragon month (April 17 to May 16) was for ambitious action. Despite the fire-versus-metal elemental drag, the Dragon's inherent energy is ambitious and large. The central theme of the Dragon month was: opportunity, even if constrained. The people who read that correctly moved.

The Metal Snake month (May 17 to June 14) is for consolidation and depth. The central theme: protect and tend. The people reading it correctly this month are not chasing anything new. They are going deep on what exists.

The Water Horse month (from June 15) is for fast movement in the direction you've been building. The central theme: acceleration. If you've been building something real, the Water Horse carries it. If you've been avoiding something difficult, the Water Horse surfaces it.

Three months. Three different central themes. Three different strategies.

## The Second Angle: Who Are You in This Particular Month?

The Mirror's second angle asks: who are you in this situation?

Applied to monthly sequencing, that question becomes: what are your natural tendencies, and how do they map onto what this specific month is asking for?

Some people are natural Dragon-month players. Bold, ambitious, comfortable with speed. Those people thrived in April. But those same tendencies are a liability in the Snake month, which rewards depth and patience over momentum. And in the Water Horse month, those tendencies are an asset again. But only if they've been disciplined enough to consolidate something real during the Snake.

Some people are natural Snake-month players. Patient, thoughtful, comfortable with depth and careful moves. They may have felt like they were falling behind in the Dragon month, missing the action. But they're doing fine right now. Their challenge for the Water Horse is to allow themselves to move fast when the energy permits.

Knowing your natural style against the month's actual demand is the second angle in practice. It's not about becoming someone you're not. It's about knowing when your defaults are helping and when they need adjustment.

## The Third Angle: What Is the Opposition Right Now?

The Mirror's third angle is the one most people skip. What is the opposition? What is actually working against you?

Applied to quarterly sequencing, the most useful version of this question is: what are you still carrying from the previous month that needs to be resolved before the next one opens?

You are sitting in the last week of the Snake month with a week before the Water Horse opens. The opposition question right now is specific.

What unresolved friction from the Snake month are you carrying into June 15? A conversation that needed to happen and didn't. A decision that was made under the Snake month's difficulty but wasn't actually right. A relationship that has been strained for three weeks without resolution.

The Water Horse is not patient. It doesn't wait for you to sort out the Snake month's debris before it starts accelerating. If the debris is there on June 15, the Water Horse picks it up and carries it. Faster and louder than the Snake month would have.

The opposition right now is: whatever you haven't resolved yet.

## The Fourth Angle: What Are You Building Toward?

The Mirror's fourth angle asks: what is the future? Where is this heading?

For quarterly sequencing, the question is: what does the Water Horse month need you to have in place before it opens?

The answer is clarity. Not perfection. Clarity. You don't need to have solved everything in the Snake month. You need to know what you're carrying forward and why.

What did you build during the Dragon month? Is it solid enough to carry into faster-moving water?

What did you protect during the Snake month? What relationships, projects, decisions are stronger now than they were on May 17?

What do you want the Water Horse month to do for you? Not vaguely. Specifically. What moves do you want to make when the energy is with you again? What are you preparing to push?

If you can answer those four questions clearly before June 15, you arrive at the Water Horse month with a plan. The Mirror doesn't guarantee the plan works. But a month as fast and volatile as the Water Horse rewards the people who arrive with one.

## The Sequencing Summary

| Month | Central Theme | What to Push | What to Hold |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------------|
| Metal Dragon (Apr 17–May 16) | Opportunity window | Bold moves, new ventures, committed bets | Overly cautious patterns |
| Metal Snake (May 17–Jun 14) | Consolidation and depth | Existing relationships, honest conversations, careful assessment | New risks, new starts |
| Water Horse (Jun 15+) | Acceleration | Prepared moves, things built during Dragon | Unresolved Snake-month friction |

The error is treating every row the same. Different months, different mandates.

The Mahjong Mirror doesn't just help you make a single decision better. It helps you sequence. To know which moment is for what kind of energy, so you stop fighting the month and start working with it.
