# Month of the Earth Pig: Who It Is Actually Lucky For

**Source:** Bill × Dave recording, 2026-11-09 (Mahjong Mirror interview, 02:32 to 09:25; raw transcript below)
**Period:** November 7 to December 7, 2026 (inside the Fire Horse year). Verified with the lunar calendar library.
**Post angle:** Everyone is calling it a lucky wealth month. Bill's question: lucky for whom? Wealth luck is personal, and it runs on your Day Master.

## The argument
- An article called this the "Golden Pig" month and a really good month for wealth. Bill's reaction: a golden pig sounds like a Metal Pig. Here the "gold" means yellow, the color of earth. The claim didn't strike him completely right.
- How do you really determine a lucky time for you? Bill falls back on Ba Zi, the standard astrology used by feng shui experts.
- In Ba Zi your **Day Master** (your day sign) tells the most about you. It has an element and an animal. The **element of your day sign** is crucial for wealth luck.
- **The rule: you are in a good wealth period when your day element controls the element of whatever period you are looking at** (hour, day, month, year).

## The control table (Bill's own list)
| Your day element | Your wealth period is a... |
|---|---|
| Wood | Earth period |
| Fire | Metal period |
| Earth | Water period |
| Metal | Wood period |
| Water | Fire period |

- This month is an Earth month, so **Wood Day Masters** are in their wealth month: making money, doing well in the job that produces money, investing.
- This year is a Fire year (the Fire Horse), so **Water Day Masters** are in their wealth year.

## The caution
- You always have to look at many things, as in *The Mahjong Mirror*: many perspectives. Your month sign has to be in harmony with this month's sign, your year sign with the year's sign. For a super big stroke of luck, winning the lottery, all the factors have to come into play.
- It works for time of day, lucky day, lucky month, lucky year.
- "When am I going to make money?" is one of the two questions Bill has been asked most often in the last 40 years.

## The Pig itself
- In Chinese culture the Pig symbolizes abundance. If your factors are aligned and you are in harmony with the Pig (the Rabbit, or the Cat in Vietnam, for example), this is likely to be a good month too. Not as promising as if it were your wealth month. A lot of signs will do well in the Pig month, both for wealth and even love and romance.
