Not every month wants the same energy from you. That's the single most useful thing I can tell you about navigating a volatile year.

The mistake most people make is picking one level of effort and holding it across the whole calendar. They push constantly and get burned when the energy shifts. Or they hold back constantly and miss the windows that were actually open.

The Mahjong Mirror is a sequencing tool.

Its first angle (what is the central theme?) applies just as well to a month as to a decision. The Dragon month's central theme was: opportunity, even if constrained. Move. The Snake month's central theme is: consolidation and depth. Go deep. The Water Horse month's central theme will be: acceleration. Be ready.

Three months. Three different mandates. You don't navigate all three the same way.
