# Month of the Metal Snake — 2026 Forecast

**Source:** Bill × Dave recording, 2026-05-23
**Period:** May 17 to Jun 14, 2026 (inside the Year of the Fire Horse)
**Post angle:** The monthly forecast — overview, who's affected, lucky/unlucky days, and three takeaways.

## Overview
The Metal Snake in a Fire Horse year is, bluntly, **inauspicious** — fire melts metal's luck, the worst elemental combination of the year. The Snake and the Horse don't get along: their pace and energy are opposites.

**Do:**
- Lay low; don't get swept up in the fast action.
- Nurture the relationships you already have — go deep on what you've got rather than chasing the new.
- Be scrupulously honest (see below).

**Avoid:**
- Taking risks.
- Starting new projects or new ventures.

## Honesty is the hinge
The single most important thing this month. Playing games or telling even a small lie — and getting caught — can lead to disastrous consequences, especially for relationships, in this high-fire year.

## Who has it easier / harder
- **Easier:** Ox and Rooster — the Snake month is one of their best of the year.
- **Hardest:** Rat — the toughest time of any sign this month.

## Health
It's deep summer and fire is at its strongest. Build in real relaxation and time to calm the mind and make peace with yourself. A difficult month for health, with genuine burnout potential.

## Travel
Fine this month — but keep it leisurely. A relaxed trip, not a whirlwind go-go itinerary.

## Money
Favor the conservative. This can actually be a good money month if you earn through **mental / intellectual** work rather than aggressive bets.

## Lucky & unlucky days
Because it's a metal month, **water days** are more favorable. Ideally pick day-signs compatible with both the Snake and the Horse:
- **Best — Sheep days.** Sheep is very compatible with both Snake and Horse. A **water Sheep day** is about as good as it gets this month.
- **Good — Rooster days,** especially a **water Rooster day.**
- **Avoid — Rat days.** Crawl in a hole; keep to relaxation, recreation, and routine work.

## Three takeaways
1. **Get R&R.**
2. **Be cautious.**
3. **Honesty is the best policy.**
