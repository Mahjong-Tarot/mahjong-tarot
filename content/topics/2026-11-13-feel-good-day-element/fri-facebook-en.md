Feel Good Friday: nobody is shut out of the money.

Every element gets its turn. So this weekend, three small things, coffee-length:

Find your day element. Your date of birth and a free Four Pillars calculator will do it.

Find your wealth element. Wood looks for Earth. Fire looks for Metal. Earth looks for Water. Metal looks for Wood. Water looks for Fire.

Mark your next turn. Find the next month with your wealth element, put it on your calendar, and write one sentence about what you want to be ready to do.

Luck isn't a club. It's a schedule.
