Not a Wood Day Master? The Pig still has something for you.

Yesterday I said the Golden Pig month isn't a wealth month for everybody. That's true. But I don't want the Pig sounding like a fraud.

In Chinese culture, the Pig symbolizes abundance. If you're in harmony with the Pig (the Rabbit, for example, or the Cat if you're Vietnamese) and your other factors line up, this is likely to be a good month for you too. Not as promising as a true wealth month. But a lot of signs will do well between now and December 7, for money and even for love and romance.

So there are two kinds of good news this month. Which one is yours?
