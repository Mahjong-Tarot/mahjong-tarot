Monday I told you the firehorse baby is the sign most likely to bring the greatest joy and the greatest aggravation. That is true. But there is no such thing as parenting "a firehorse."

Your child is four pillars, not one. Year, month, day, and more -- each layer stacks on the last. A baby born this month is already a double horse. But are they born on a Water day, which cools the fire? An Earth day, which grounds it? Those details matter. They produce meaningfully different children.

I spent ten years as a nanny, two boys, same house, opposite approaches. The coddled one needed the buggy ride before every nap. The other one was laid down and left to find his own way. Both did fine. What would not have worked: applying one method to both.

The Mahjong Mirror's whole discipline is this: look closer and customize. You do not apply a template to every situation. You study the actual situation in front of you and respond to that.

Great parenting is great coaching. A good coach does not use the same play for every player. They understand how this player is different.
