## Chapter 17 (original 33) — pilot

Chinese (Wang Bi): 知人者智，自知者明。勝人者有力，自勝者強。知足者富。強行者有志。不失其所者久。死而不亡者壽。

### Verse

| # | Status | Original | New | Test | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 17.1 | ✅ | Knowing others shows discernment. / Knowing yourself shows wisdom. | — | Round trip passed ("discernment" came back as "understanding"; meaning intact). 8 words vs Legge 13. | 智 → discernment, 明 → wisdom. Wang Bi: self-knowledge "surpasses 智", so the ascending order is right. |
| 17.2 | ✏️ | Defeating others shows strength. / Mastering yourself shows **superior strength of inner power**. | Defeating others shows strength. / Mastering yourself shows **greater strength**. | Brevity: 12 words → 9 (Legge 12). Round trip passed. | 有力 / 強 is a plain contrast of force vs. strength. "Superior strength of inner power" stacks three near-synonyms and adds "inner", which the characters do not contain. |
| 17.3a | ✅ | Being satisfied with what you have is true wealth. | — | 9 words (Legge 9). | 知足者富, accurate. |
| 17.3b | ⚠️ | Persevering is a sign of inner strength. | Steady effort shows purpose. | Accuracy. Round trip passed ("continuous effort shows purpose"). 7 → 4 words (Legge 11). | 強行者有志: 志 is will/purpose, not strength. "Inner strength" repeats 17.2 and mistranslates 志. Wang Bi reads 強行 positively ("practice it diligently and the will is attained"); some translators read it as a warning against forcing. Followed Wang Bi and Bill's positive reading. **Bill to confirm the positive reading.** |
| 17.4a | ⚠️ | Remaining in the flow of the natural order while fulfilling responsibilities is a key to long life. | Whoever stays where they belong lasts long. | Accuracy, brevity. 17 → 7 words (Legge 12). Round trip passed ("He who stays in his place has a long life"). | 不失其所者久: "does not lose their place". "Fulfilling responsibilities" is Legge's Confucian gloss ("requirements of his position"), not in the characters. Wang Bi: see yourself clearly, act within your measure, do not lose your place. "Where they belong" keeps it Daoist (ziran) and plain. **Bill to confirm "where they belong" over "their place".** |
| 17.4b | ⚠️ | Having influenced many and produced enduring wisdom is a key to being forever alive in the minds of people. | Whoever dies but whose Dao lives on has true long life. | Accuracy, brevity. 19 → 11 words (Legge 11). Round trip passed ("live on in the Tao will indeed have a long life"). | 死而不亡者壽: "dies but does not perish". Wang Bi: the body dies but the Dao one lived by is not lost. Bill's line adds "influenced many", "enduring wisdom", "minds of people", none of which are in the text, and turns a statement about Dao into one about reputation. Alternative that also passed the round trip and is closer to Bill's intent: "Whoever dies but is not forgotten has true long life." **Bill to choose.** |

All five tightened to the length of the applications in chapters 1–10 (one to two sentences). Each keeps Bill's format: bold lead phrase, then direct advice.
