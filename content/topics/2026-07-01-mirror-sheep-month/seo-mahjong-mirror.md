## FAQ Schema

**Q: What is the Mahjong Mirror Second Angle in relationships?**
A: The Mahjong Mirror's Second Angle is Know Thyself. Applied to relationships, it asks one question before any big conversation or decision: who are you right now in this relationship? Not who you want to be, but who you actually are. This honest self-assessment is the foundation for any clear love decision.

**Q: What is the Mahjong Mirror Third Angle in love?**
A: The Third Angle is the Opposition. In love readings, the opposition is often external: circumstance gaps, distance, incompatibility. But in a high-passion month like the Wood Sheep month, where two fire signs coincide, the opposition is frequently the reader's own emotional reaction speed. Responding before you've thought, escalating before you know what you want: that's the real opposition to name.

**Q: How do you tell a soulmate moment from a hot-headed mistake?**
A: Three steps from the Mahjong Mirror: First, empty the cup. Clear any unprocessed heat from previous months before making a decision. Second, apply the Second Angle (Know Thyself): write down who you actually are in this relationship right now, and what you want most from the Soulmate Month. Third, apply the Third Angle (Opposition): name your own emotional pattern in conflict. With those two answers in hand, you have a compass for the month. Any action that moves toward what you named in step two is aligned. Any action driven by the temperature of the moment is heat, not clarity.

**Q: What are the Mahjong Mirror's four angles?**
A: The four angles are: (1) Central Theme: what is this situation really about; (2) Know Thyself: who are you in this situation; (3) The Opposition: what is genuinely blocking progress; (4) The Future: where does this path lead. Bill Hajdu's book, The Mahjong Mirror, walks through all four with practical exercises.

**Q: Is the Mahjong Mirror only for romantic decisions?**
A: No. The Mahjong Mirror is a decision-making framework applicable to any major life decision: career, finances, relationships, transitions. The four angles work the same way regardless of the topic. In the Wood Sheep month, romantic decisions are the most likely surface where the framework is needed, but the method transfers anywhere.
