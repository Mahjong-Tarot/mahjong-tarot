I'm the Firepig. On Monday I told you the Soulmate Month isn't as safe as its name suggests, and that the Peach Blossom is turning up in the problem position more than any other month this year. That's the diagnosis. Today I want to give you the actual tool, because knowing a risk exists and knowing what to do about it are two different things.

Two cards keep showing up in my readings this month. The Peach. The Sword. Neither one is comfortable to see land on the table in front of you. Both of them, read correctly, tell you exactly where you stand.

<h2>What the Peach Actually Means</h2>

The Peach: the card of romantic temptation and affairs. People hear that and assume it means someone is already cheating. Sometimes it does. More often, especially this month, it means the fire is looking for somewhere to go and hasn't decided where yet.

Double fire creates passion that has to be spent. If it's being spent at home, on the person you're with, the Peach can actually show up in a good position, meaning the passion is real and it's pointed correctly. When it shows up in the problem position, it's telling you the passion exists but it isn't landing where it should. That's a warning to act on now, not a verdict to panic over.

I've been surprised myself at how often this card has come up this month. It's not subtle. When it lands in the problem spot, I don't soften it for the person across the table, and I won't soften it here either. It means: look at where your attention is actually going, honestly, before you decide there's nothing to look at.

<h2>What the Sword Actually Means</h2>

The Sword is a different kind of message. Where the Peach points at temptation, the Sword points at a cut that needs to be made. It shows up when something in a relationship isn't working and the honest move is to end it, not to keep patching it.

This month, the Sword and the Peach sometimes show up together, and that combination tells its own story. A relationship that's already been drifting, combined with temptation elsewhere, isn't usually a sign to fight harder for the original relationship out of guilt. It's often a sign that the fight has already been lost, and the fire is simply confirming what both people already suspect.

I know that's not the answer people want to hear during what's supposed to be the most romantic month of the year. But the Mirror doesn't exist to tell you what you want to hear. It exists to show you what's actually there.

<h2>The Second Angle: Know Thyself</h2>

Here's where the Mahjong Mirror framework gives you something to do with these two cards, whether you've had a reading or not.

The Second Angle asks a simple question: who are you in this relationship right now? Not who you want to be. Not who you were a year ago. Who you actually are, today, in the middle of a month running on double fire.

Are you bringing the passion home? Are you the one who's distracted, restless, looking somewhere else even if you haven't acted on it? Know Thyself isn't about judgment. It's about honesty before anything else happens that you can't undo. Most people skip this step because it's uncomfortable. This month, skipping it is the expensive mistake.

<h2>The Third Angle: The Opposition</h2>

The Third Angle asks what's actually standing in the way. Not the surface argument about who forgot to call, who's been working late, who said something sharp last week. The real opposition underneath it.

In a two-fire month, the real opposition is often simpler than people want it to be. It's pace. It's pride. It's one partner running hotter than the other and neither one saying so out loud. Name the real obstacle, and you can work with it. Keep fighting about the surface argument, and you'll be having the same fight in September with nothing resolved.

<blockquote>Chemistry tells you how you feel. The Mirror tells you what's actually there.</blockquote>

<h2>Putting It Together</h2>

If the Peach lands in your problem position this month, don't panic and don't ignore it. Use the Second Angle. Ask honestly where your attention has been going.

If the Sword shows up alongside it, use the Third Angle. Ask what's really been standing between you and your partner, not the excuse you've been giving each other.

The Soulmate Month is real. The opportunity in it is real. But the same fire that can take a relationship to the next level can just as easily expose the one that was already cracking. The cards don't create that outcome. They just show you which one you're already living.
