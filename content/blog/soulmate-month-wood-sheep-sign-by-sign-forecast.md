I'm the Firepig. And I've been waiting to write this one.

The last two months were rough. The Metal Snake month and the Water Horse month both carried difficult elemental combinations: fire melting metal's luck, the water and fire clash adding tension on top. A lot of people have been white-knuckling it through June. I get it.

But that changes on July 7.

On July 7, the Wood Sheep month opens. And in 35 years of doing this work, I don't throw the word "soulmate" around casually. This month earns it.

## Why the Wood Sheep Month Is Different

Here's the mechanics. The month sign and the year sign are soulmates. When that alignment happens, it creates a window where major life events tend to cluster. Not minor ones. The big ones. Engagements. Reconciliations. The conversation you've been putting off for months. The relationship that finally moves. These moments have to land somewhere in a year. The Soulmate Month is where they tend to land.

The element story makes it even stronger. Wood feeds fire. The month element is wood, the year element is fire, and wood feeds fire is one of the most auspicious elemental combinations you can have. The chemistry between the two Chinese signs is high. Both are favorable.

Then there's the flavor of the month itself. The Sheep is the feminine sign. It's the sign of emotions and of love. The Horse is fire. The Sheep is also fire. Two fire signs together means passion. A lot of passion.

July is the month for lovers.

If you want to deepen an existing relationship, this is your window. If you want to take one to the next level, this is your window. If you're looking to find someone, this is your window. The stars don't hand you many months like this in a year, and in a Fire Horse year, this is the one.

But here's the caveat I always have to give: where passion runs high, tempers run high. Two fire signs together means exactly that. This is a month to deepen connections, not to spark fights. Self-control isn't optional this month. It's the whole game.

## One More Thing Before Your Sign

I need to say this clearly. This is a Fire Horse year and a Sheep month. That means I can't read it as a generic "good Sheep month" for everyone. The nuance below blends both the year energy and the month energy. As a result, no sign is truly great. About half are favorable. About half are challenged. Find your sign below and read it honestly.

---

## The 12 Signs: Your Wood Sheep Month Forecast

### Horse, Pig, and Rabbit (Cat in Vietnamese)
**Most favorable. Be confident, but not reckless.**

These three are traditionally the strongest signs in a Sheep month. If you're one of them, you can look for success in love and lead with confidence. But don't go hog wild. Even the most favorable positioning in a Soulmate Month doesn't mean life hands you everything easy. Some soulmate moments are joyful. Some are hard lessons. Don't get swept up in the high-speed turmoil the Fire Horse generates. Passion plus confidence is powerful. Passion plus carelessness turns the month against you.

### Dragon
**Good. High-flying energy with wood on your side.**

The Dragon and Horse make good music together. Wood is the Dragon's fixed element, so the month's wood energy works directly in your favor. You can move forward, take a little risk, and make things happen. The key is not to take on too much. This is a month to move with purpose, not to stack every project and commitment on top of each other.

### Dog
**Good. One of your stronger months in a strong year.**

The Dog does great in a Horse year, and it holds up reasonably well in a Sheep month too. You're in a favorable position. Move forward with intention, stay relational, and trust the month's energy. The Dog's loyalty and steadiness are genuine assets right now.

### Snake
**Quietly good. Passionate romance is in reach.**

The Snake and Sheep get along well. And because the month is wood, the wood feeds the Snake's fixed fire energy, which opens the door to passionate romance. Snake and Horse isn't the most favorable year pairing, so some caution is still warranted. But Snakes tend to be wise and read situations well. If you're paying attention and not forcing things, this should be a good month.

### Tiger
**Be careful with relationships.**

Tiger gets along well with the Horse, so the year sign is friendly. But Tiger and Sheep don't make natural partners. The Sheep's emotional, loyal energy may feel a little too quiet and domestic for the Tiger, who needs to feel seen and energized. Relationships could be difficult this month. Be aware of that gap. If you're in a partnership, make a deliberate effort to show up for the other person.

### Rat
**Tend your garden.**

The Rat already faces its most challenging year in the Fire Horse. The Sheep's emotional, clingy side isn't a natural match either. This month, the Rat needs to lean into what it already has. Tend the garden. Do the daily work for the people you care about. This isn't a month to look for new romantic beginnings. It's a month to hold what matters.

### Ox
**Put real effort into existing relationships.**

Ox Horse isn't great. The Ox dislikes the high speed of the year, and the Horse doesn't naturally appreciate the Sheep's emotionally demanding side. The Ox could have a difficult month. Like the Rat, the move here is to put genuine effort into existing relationships rather than stretching toward new ones. Don't let the passion of the month pull you into fights you don't need.

### Monkey
**Avoid new relationships; tend what you have.**

The Monkey doesn't click with either the Sheep or the Horse. And the Monkey's fixed metal element actually destroys the month's wood luck. That's a double challenge. Probably not a good time to start a new relationship. And with tempers running hot across the board, the Monkey needs to resist the urge to play games. Be serious, be committed, be present. The Monkey who tends its garden this month comes out ahead. The one who looks for shortcuts doesn't.

### Rooster
**Hold back the urge to argue.**

The Rooster is not terrible with the Horse, but it doesn't click with the Sheep. And like the Monkey, the Rooster is a metal sign. Its metal energy can destroy the month's wood luck. Roosters have a natural tendency to get into arguments, and some of them almost enjoy it. This month, that habit could cause real damage. Summer heat plus high tempers plus a Rooster's sharp tongue is a combination that can undo months of good relationship work in a single evening. Hold back. It's restraining, yes. But letting heat win would be the real loss.

## The One Rule That Holds for Every Sign

No matter where your sign falls in that breakdown, one rule holds.

Lead with self-control.

The Wood Sheep month brings passion and it brings life events. Some of those events will be wonderful. Some will be difficult. The ones who navigate this month best are the ones who brought intention into it, not just heat.

The month's energy will do its job. Your job is to not let the temperature push you into something you'll regret.

July 7 is four days away. You have time to decide what kind of month you want to walk into.

In over 35 years of doing readings, the months I see produce the most meaningful love outcomes are the ones where people went in with both passion and clarity. The Wood Sheep month is exactly the kind of window where a single clear conversation can change everything.
