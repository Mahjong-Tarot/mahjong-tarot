# Month of the Wood Sheep

*Recorded June 29, 2026. Covers the lunar month running July 7 to August 7, inside the year of the Fire Horse.*

## Why this month is special

This month is the month of the Wood Sheep, and it stands out for two reasons.

First, it is the yearly Soulmate Month, where the month sign and the year sign are soulmates. When that happens, many people will have at least one of their most important events, situations, or life changes of the entire year occur during this window. It is a significant time.

Second, the element interplay is unusually favorable. Wood and Horse have an extremely high compatibility rating. The month element is wood and the year element is fire, and wood feeds fire, which is a highly auspicious combination. Both Chinese signs are favorable.

## The flavor of the month: love and fire

The Sheep (also called the Goat) is the feminine sign, and it is the sign of emotions and of love. The Sheep is a fire sign, the same as the Horse. With two fire signs together, expect lots and lots of passion.

July is the month for lovers. It is probably the best time of the year to take a relationship to the next level, to deepen an existing one, or to look for someone new. It is all good.

The one caution: when passions run high, tempers can run high too. With both Goat and Horse being fire signs, avoid fights with anyone, and especially with the people you care about, your partner, boyfriend, or girlfriend. Self-control matters a lot this month and this year.

## An important caveat on reading the signs

This is a Fire Horse year and a Sheep month, so it is not accurate to read it as a generic "good Sheep month." A sign that normally does well in the Sheep month may still struggle if the year itself has been hard on it. The nuance below blends both the year sign (Horse) and the month sign (Sheep).

A general note worth keeping in mind: no sign is truly "great" this month. The Horse, Pig, and Rabbit do best in a Sheep month, but none of the three is great in a Horse year. Signs are generally not at their best in their own year. So this is more of a mixed bag, with roughly half the signs favorable and half challenged.

## Sign-by-sign guidance

**Horse, Pig, and Rabbit (Cat in Vietnamese) — most favorable, but with care.**
Traditionally the strongest signs in a Sheep month. Be confident and look for success, but do not go hog wild. Even favorable signs need care, because some soulmate situations and life lessons are hard lessons. Do not get caught up in the high-speed turmoil the Horse generates.

**Dragon and Dog — also good.**
The Dog does great in a Horse year and not too bad in a Sheep month. The Dragon and Horse make good music together, a high-flying combination, and wood is good for the Dragon since wood is the Dragon's fixed element. Both can move forward, take a little risk, be confident, and make things happen, while being careful not to take on too much.

**Snake — quietly good.**
Snake and Sheep get along well. Since it is a wood month, wood feeds the Snake's fixed fire energy, opening the door to passionate romance. Snake Horse is not the most favorable year pairing, so some caution is warranted, but Snakes tend to be wise and read situations well, so this should be a good month for them.

**Tiger — be careful with relationships.**
Tiger gets along well with the Horse, so the year sign matches nicely. But Tiger and Sheep are not adventurous or bold enough for the Tiger, and the Tiger may not get the attention it needs, so relationships could be difficult this month.

**Rat — nurture relationships.**
The Rat already faces its most challenging year with the Horse, and it does not feel good about the Sheep's emotionalism. Rats need to really nurture their relationships this month. Tend your garden and do your best for the people you care about.

**Ox — nurture relationships.**
Ox Horse is not great; the Ox dislikes the high speed of the year, and the Horse does not like the Sheep's clingy, emotional side. The Ox could have a difficult month and, like the Rat, needs to put real effort into existing relationships.

**Monkey — be careful, especially with new relationships.**
The Monkey does not click with either the Sheep or the Horse, and the Monkey's fixed metal element destroys the month's wood luck. Like the Rat, the Monkey shares a compatibility group with it and must tend its garden. Probably not a good time to start a new relationship. With tempers running hot, the Monkey should resist playing "monkey games" and instead be serious, understanding, and committed.

**Rooster — hold back the urge to argue.**
The Rooster is not terrible with the Horse but does not click with the Sheep. Like the Monkey, the Rooster is a metal sign, so its metal can also destroy the month's wood luck. Roosters have a tendency to get into arguments and almost enjoy them, so it is important to hold that back. It may feel restraining, but letting summer heat and hot tempers win could cause real trouble in relationships.

## Bottom line

It is a passionate, love-charged month with major life events likely. About half the signs are favorable (Horse, Pig, Rabbit, Dragon, Dog, Snake) and about half are challenged (Rat, Ox, Tiger, Monkey, Rooster). Whatever your sign, lead with self-control: this is a month to deepen connection, not to spark fights.
