Somebody is going to sell you 2027 as a sweet, gentle, romantic year.

You'll see it in the forecasts. Love in the air. Beauty. Softness. A nice long exhale after the Fire Horse. And the people selling it won't be lying to you. They'll just be leaving out the half that costs you something.

I'm the Firepig. In over 35 years of doing readings, I've learned that every sign has a good side and a dark side, and that everybody wants to hear about the good side all the time. The big names in this business know it. Everything they put out is brimming with possibilities.

I'd rather look at the dark side. Not because I'm gloomy. Because the dark side is the part that catches you off guard, and what catches you off guard is what costs you.

The Fire Sheep arrives on February 6, 2027. Here's what the brochure leaves out.

## The Number-One Problem Has Four Names

Emotional. Whiny. Clingy. Needy.

That's the Sheep's dark side in four words, and next year it won't stay politely inside the people born under the sign. It's the flow of the whole year. This year was a year of action. Next year is a year of emotions, and emotions are a lot less fun when they belong to somebody who won't stop sharing them.

People around you are going to feel more and need more. They'll lean harder and hurt faster. The coworker who used to be fine will not be fine. The friend who used to text you once will text you four times and then ask if you're upset with her.

> The Sheep can be darling. The Sheep can also drive you up the wall. Next year you get both.

None of that is a disaster on its own. It's weather. But if you walk into it expecting sunshine because somebody promised you a gentle year, you're going to be standing in the rain wondering what you did wrong.

## If You Were Born in a Rat Year, I'm Sorry

Rat people are going to have a really hard time with this.

My son Dave is a Rat, and I told him plainly: you're going to be aggravated next year. I'll tell you the same thing if that's your sign. People around you are going to be showing exactly the behavior that gets under your skin, and it's going to go on for twelve months.

I'm not telling you this to scare you. I'm telling you so that when it starts, you recognize it. The year isn't personally out to annoy you. It only feels that way.

## Think You're Too Rational for This?

Here's the part nobody expects.

If you're the logical, rational kind of person, you may feel quite uncomfortable next year. That much you probably guessed. You're going to be surrounded by feelings, and you're going to wish everybody would just get to the point.

What you may not have guessed is that it could be you.

You may be the rational type and still catch yourself getting too emotional. Too involved. Losing your self-control and not being in charge of situations the way you normally are. The person who always keeps a cool head may find next year that the head isn't so cool, and nobody is more surprised than they are.

That can throw a real monkey wrench in your plans. Your own behavior first. Then, especially, the behavior of the people around you.

A word to the wise: the person most at risk from a year of emotions is the one who's sure it doesn't apply to them.

## It Looks Soft. It Isn't.

This is where the brochure really falls apart.

The Sheep is a Yin sign, relatively passive. So people assume it's harmless. But the Sheep is also a fire sign, and 2027 is a fire year. Even a passive sign can do reckless things when it's sitting on that much fire.

So this isn't only about feelings. It's about self-control in general.

And the place recklessness does the most damage is money. The Sheep isn't necessarily good at making it, but it's sure good at spending it. We're all going to have a tendency to overspend and stretch ourselves too thin. Add the fact that the Sheep has very little interest in planning, and you've got a problem I'm going to spend all of Wednesday's post on.

The Sheep doesn't charge at trouble the way the Horse does. It just stops paying attention until trouble is standing in the living room.

## And Then There Are the Sheep People Themselves

Everything so far is about the year. There's also the person.

If you love someone born in a Sheep year, you already know some of this. Sheep people can be sensitive to the point of being a big crybaby. They often need something to light their fire and a push to get moving. I don't want to say lazy. They're just not go-getters by nature. And even when they know exactly what they're doing, they still want a shoulder to lean on.

Not everyone born in a Sheep year is like that. The more your other signs strengthen your Sheep sign, the more likely you are to see it in yourself.

They can also be some of the sweetest people you'll ever meet, which is why you put up with it. I'll give you some help with that on Friday.

## What to Do With This Before February

You have a little under four months left before the Sheep takes over. Use them to get honest.

Ask yourself who in your life is about to get harder to deal with, and decide now how much patience you're willing to spend. Ask yourself whether you're the one who thinks this doesn't apply to you. If so, it applies to you.

Then work on the one skill that covers every item on this list. Self-control. Over your temper, your feelings and your wallet. When the heat comes up, [walk away first and answer later](/blog/posts/feel-good-friday-walk-away-first-answer-later). Practice it before you need it.

I've told you before that the [Fire Sheep year has real gifts in it](/blog/posts/fire-sheep-2027-love-passion-builds), and it does. But nobody ever got hurt by the part of a year they saw coming.
