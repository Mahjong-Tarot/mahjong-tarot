## FAQ Schema

**Q: What is the weekend challenge for the Soulmate Month?**
A: Two moves before July 7 begins. First: name one relationship to deepen this month. Not fix or repair, but actively deepen. Second: name one specific fight you're going to refuse to pick. Write both down. That's the plan for the Wood Sheep Soulmate Month.

**Q: Why does self-control matter so much in the Wood Sheep month?**
A: Two fire signs together (Sheep and Horse) create high passion and high tempers. The energy that makes the Soulmate Month the best love window of the year is the same energy that can produce serious arguments. Deciding in advance which fight you'll skip, before the heat of the moment makes the decision for you, is how you protect the relationships the month is asking you to deepen.

**Q: What is the difference between fixing a relationship and deepening one?**
A: Fixing comes from a position of something being broken. Deepening comes from a position of something worth building on. The Soulmate Month is a month of connection and emotional amplification. It works best when directed at what is genuinely worth more investment, not at repairing what is fundamentally not working.

**Q: Can the Soulmate Month challenge work for non-romantic relationships?**
A: Yes. The Wood Sheep month amplifies all genuine emotional connections, not only romantic ones. The Sheep is the sign of emotional connection and relational warmth in general. Choosing one friendship, family relationship, or partnership to consciously deepen during this window is a valid and valuable use of the month's energy.
