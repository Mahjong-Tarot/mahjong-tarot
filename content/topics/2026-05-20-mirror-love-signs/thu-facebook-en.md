There are two tiles that show up repeatedly in Fire Horse year love readings: the Peach Blossom and the Sword. Neither one is comfortable. Both are clarifying.

The thing about the Third Angle of the Mahjong Mirror is that it asks for honesty at a level most people avoid. Not "what do I want?" That's the First Angle. Not "who am I?" That's the Second Angle. The Third Angle asks: what is standing in the way?

And the answer is almost never what people expect. It's usually a pattern that has been running quietly for years. The Fire Horse year isn't creating the pattern. It's making the pattern impossible to ignore.

The useful move this week is to ask the Third Angle question yourself before the year asks it for you. What is the structural obstacle in your love life right now? Not the argument, not the habit. The thing underneath.
