The Mahjong Mirror's Third Angle is the one most people resist. It asks you to name what's actually opposing you in your love life. Not what you're fighting about. What's underneath it.

Two tiles keep appearing in love readings this year: the Peach Blossom and the Sword. Both make people uncomfortable. Both are clarifying.

The Peach Blossom isn't just about temptation. It's about an unmet need. When it shows up, the question isn't "is something happening?" The question is: what does your relationship not provide that is making outside attention feel like an answer?

The Sword isn't just about endings. It points to something that has been allowed to continue too long and needs to stop. A pattern. A dynamic. A story you've been telling yourself that stopped being true a while ago.

The Third Angle asks the hard question because the hard question is the useful one. This year, that question is being asked whether you hold up the mirror or not.
