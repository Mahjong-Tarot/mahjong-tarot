5Ds Plan · iPhone app v1 (slim)

The Mahjong Tarot, on your iPhone

A customer-only iPhone app that mirrors the website: a free side where anyone sees their own chart and the daily almanac, and a member side where people start a free trial or sign in to read their reports.

Customer app only, no admin iPhone only Rev 4 · 14 Nov 2026 Owner: Dave
1

Define the problem

Bill's readers live on their phones, but everything they get from us lives on a website.

  • Who feels it: two groups. Visitors who arrive from social posts and want "what does today mean for me", and members (yearly $99, lifetime $299, or a 90-day free trial) who come back for their reports.
  • What it costs: the daily almanac is the habit-forming feature, but a website has no home-screen icon and no reminder, so people forget to come back. Members sign in again in a browser to find a report they already have. Every lapsed visit is a lost chance to turn a free chart into a membership.
  • Why now: the 12 Nov redesign put a free chart on the website (/chart, "one angle of the mirror") and a clear membership offer (/membership). The app puts that same first step on the home screen. 2027 (Fire Sheep year) forecasts are the next reason to come back.

Design the workflow

Four tabs: Today, My Chart, Reports, Me. Today and My Chart are free. Reports lead to a free trial or sign-in.

Free path

Open app→Birth date, birth time (optional), gender (with a time)→My Chart→Today's almanac→Come back daily

Not a member yet

Tap Reports or "There is more to your chart"→Start 90-day free trial→Name, email, password (birth details prefilled)→Confirm email→Purple Star + Relationship unlocked

Member path

Tap Reports→Sign in (password or magic link)→Reports list→Read or run a reading

How it gets built

  1. Phase A, free side: Welcome, Today, My Chart, on TestFlight. Shippable on its own.
  2. Phase B, members: Start free trial, Sign in, Universal Links for magic-link and confirm emails, Purple Star and Relationship reports, Me.
  3. Phase C, App Store: icon, screenshots, privacy labels and review, submitted from the GitHub workflow.

Determine the ROI

FAST goal: within 90 days of launch, 1,000 people have their chart on their iPhone and 15% of them open Today at least 3 days a week.

Success measures

  • Installs and charts created (free activation).
  • Weekly returning users on Today (the habit).
  • Free trials started inside the app (signup_source = 'app'), and how many of those join yearly or lifetime when the trial ends (the conversion).
  • Relationship readings run per member (the feature most likely to bring a second person in).

Return

Membership is $99 a year or $299 for life, after a 90-day free trial. A handful of conversions a month pays for upkeep; the bigger return is a daily touchpoint with Bill's work that the website can't give. Costs: build time for phases A–C. The Talent Edge Apple account ($99/year) already exists and is shared across apps; Expo's cloud builds start free, and any paid plan is shared with Forever and PPC.

5

Definition of Done

  • A new user enters a birth date, with or without a time, and sees the same chart as the website's /chart for those details (spot-checked on 10 known charts, half without a time).
  • Today matches /almanac/[date] for any date, signed in or not.
  • A free user starts the 90-day trial inside the app, confirms their email, and the same account works on the website.
  • A member signs in with password or magic link, sees only their own reports, reads their Purple Star, and reads and runs relationship readings.
  • No payment happens inside the app; the trial-ending email links to /membership.
  • Account deletion works from the Me tab.
  • Live on the App Store under the Talent Edge Apple account, with the website linking to it.
  • Store builds come from the GitHub workflow on EAS; a JavaScript-only fix reaches phones as an EAS Update; a commit that only touches mobile/ doesn't rebuild the website.
  • Tapping a magic-link or confirm email on an iPhone with the app installed opens the app, not Safari.
  • Bill and the team know: the app is a companion to the site, membership is bought on the website, and content updates (almanac, reports) need no app release.

Decisions (14 Nov)

  1. Free chart asks for birth date and birth time; time is optional.
  2. The 90-day free trial can be started inside the app.
  3. Launch reports: Purple Star and Relationship. The others follow in v1.1.
  4. Technology: the shared mobile standard. Expo, iPhone only, Talent Edge Apple account, cloud builds.
Wireframes are low-fidelity: layout and content only, in the website's current type (Libre Franklin, Inter, EB Garamond for Chinese characters) and tokens. Chart and almanac values are placeholders.
